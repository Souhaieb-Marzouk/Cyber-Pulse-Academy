How it works

Pick a topic

Browse a catalog or search by name, ATT&CK id or tag. Reading is always open, with no gate and no sign-up.

Set a name and country

Once, before your first exercise. Stored only in your browser, never sent unless the owner switches statistics to remote mode, and never the raw value.

Work the three batches

Detection and triage, then response and hands-on configuration, then the adversarial batch written from the attacker side. Different angles, same subject.

See where you actually stand

Every batch is scored against a pass mark, your results stay on your device, and the public statistics show only anonymous aggregates.
