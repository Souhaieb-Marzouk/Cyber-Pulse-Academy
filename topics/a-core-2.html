In more detail

CompTIA A+ Core 2 (V15) is a vendor certification offered by CompTIA. This independent study guide covers all 4 objective domains: Operating systems, Security, Software troubleshooting, Operational procedures. Each domain is broken into 11 lessons in total, and every lesson has a MEDIUM and a HARD scenario exam. The exams use realistic workplace situations rather than recall questions, so you practise applying each objective the way the job asks for it.
