In more detail

AI Essentials is a vendor certification offered by CompTIA. This independent study guide covers all 1 objective domains: Objectives. Each domain is broken into 11 lessons in total, and every lesson has a MEDIUM and a HARD scenario exam. The exams use realistic workplace situations rather than recall questions, so you practise applying each objective the way the job asks for it.

Yes. The page opens with a plain-language section written at B1 English level: a simple definition, why the topic matters, how it works in a few steps, a real-world example and the key takeaways. The detailed technical explanation comes after it, for readers who already work in security.

How hard are the AI Essentials exercises on Cyber Pulse Academy?

The exercises are graded extreme. They are hands-on scenario work rather than definitions: reading logs and alerts, choosing the right next step, configuring or correcting something, and explaining your reasoning. Most exercises use a pass mark of about 70 percent.
