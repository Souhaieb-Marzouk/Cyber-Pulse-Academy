DOP-C02 AWS Certified DevOps Engineer - Professional (DOP-C02)

DOP-C02AWSExtremeCertificationUnofficial Study Resource

In more detail

AWS Certified DevOps Engineer - Professional (DOP-C02) is a professional-level certification for practitioners who design and operate complex AWS estates. The exam is organised into 6 content domains: SDLC Automation (22%), Configuration Management and IaC (17%), Resilient Cloud Solutions (15%), Monitoring and Logging (15%), Incident and Event Response (14%), Security and Compliance (17%). It is delivered as 65 scored questions and 10 unscored, scored against a scaled pass mark of 750 out of 1,000 with no per-domain minimum, so strength in one domain offsets weakness in another. Every task statement in this topic carries a MEDIUM and a HARD exercise batch, so the practice material follows the published outline of the exam rather than a general reading list. The statements here are taken from the exam guide, and the task list is the one AWS publishes: if AWS revises the guide, this topic is revised with it.
