CompTIA Data+

CompTIAExtremeCertificationUnofficial Study Resource

In more detail

CompTIA Data+ is a vendor certification offered by CompTIA. This independent study guide covers all 5 objective domains: Data concepts and environments, Data acquisition and preparation, Data analysis, Visualization and reporting, Data governance. Each domain is broken into 18 lessons in total, and every lesson has a MEDIUM and a HARD scenario exam. The exams use realistic workplace situations rather than recall questions, so you practise applying each objective the way the job asks for it.
