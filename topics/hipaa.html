HIPAA

GRC & ComplianceVery hardKeyword

HIPAA is the United States federal law that governs the privacy and security of protected health information, and its security obligations are expressed through the Security Rule together with the Privacy Rule, the Breach Notification Rule and the Transactions and Code Sets Rule. The Security Rule is deliberately flexible and technology-neutral: it requires covered entities and their business associates to implement administrative, physical and technical safeguards that are reasonable and appropriate for their size, complexity and risk, and it sorts implementation specifications into required and addressable categories, where addressable means the organisation must either implement the specification or document why an equivalent alternative is reasonable and appropriate. The technical safeguards include access control, audit controls, integrity controls, person or entity authentication and transmission security, which in practice means unique user identification, role-based authorisation, emergency access procedures, automatic logoff, encryption in transit and at rest, and immutable audit logging of systems that touch electronic protected health information. Risk analysis is not optional and is the foundation of every other decision; a documented, current risk analysis tied to a remediation plan is the single artefact most often probed in an investigation. Business associate agreements extend obligations down the supply chain, and breach notification has a four-part risk assessment with defined timelines. Practitioners should treat ePHI data flow mapping as the prerequisite for meaningful segmentation and monitoring.

What you will practise

  • You will be able to separate the Privacy Rule, Security Rule, Breach Notification Rule and Transactions Rule obligations.
  • You will be able to classify an implementation specification as required or addressable and document the decision either way.
  • You will be able to perform and record a risk analysis that links threats, vulnerabilities and ePHI systems to remediation.
  • You will be able to implement technical safeguards including unique identification, emergency access and audit controls.
  • You will be able to run the four-factor breach risk assessment and determine notification obligations.
  • You will be able to manage business associate agreements and inherited responsibilities across a supply chain.
