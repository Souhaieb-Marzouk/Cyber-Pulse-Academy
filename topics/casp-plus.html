Simple definition

This is a high level security certification from CompTIA, an organisation that publishes vendor neutral information technology exams. The code is CAS-005 and the current name is SecurityX. It replaced an older exam called CompTIA Advanced Security Practitioner, or CASP+, and it is aimed at people with several years of security experience.

Why should you care?

Senior security jobs are not about knowing one tool; they are about making good decisions when money and time are limited. This certificate proves you can design a secure architecture, weigh risk and explain your choices to managers. It is often used to meet seniority requirements for government and defence contract work. For your career it signals readiness for architect or lead level responsibility rather than day to day administration.

How it works, in plain steps

  1. Check that you meet the recommended experience first, because this exam assumes you have already worked in security for several years.
  2. Study the five domains in order, and for each one link the theory to a real project you have worked on.
  3. Practise performance based tasks, where you configure or analyse something on screen instead of choosing an answer.
  4. Read architecture case studies and write short design notes, so you practise justifying a control choice.
  5. Work through practice questions to find weak domains, then revisit the underlying concepts rather than memorising answers.
  6. Book the exam when you can explain every domain to a colleague without notes.

Real-world example

Picture a hospital that wants doctors to reach patient records from home. A junior engineer asks which firewall to buy. A SecurityX level engineer asks different questions first. Who is allowed to see which records? What happens if a laptop is stolen? How do we prove later who opened a file? The certificate shows you can start with the business need and the risk, then choose technology that fits both, instead of reaching for a product name.

Key takeaways

  • This is an expert exam, so plan for significant study time on top of existing job experience.
  • Every domain rewards judgement about risk and trade-offs, not the recall of product features.
  • Confirm the current exam objectives and question format on the vendor website, since CASP+ was retired and replaced.

In more detail

CompTIA SecurityX, exam code CAS-005, is the advanced, expert level security certification that replaced CompTIA Advanced Security Practitioner, or CASP+, in 2024. It targets senior security engineers, security architects and lead analysts who already hold a mid level certification and now design, integrate and govern security across an organisation. The exam tests judgement as much as knowledge, mixing multiple choice and performance based items in which you perform a task in a simulated environment. Passing it shows an employer that you can take a business requirement, choose controls, weigh risk and defend the decision to technical and non technical audiences. It supports roles such as security architect, senior security engineer and security consultant. The domain structure below follows the published objectives for CAS-005; confirm the current version with the vendor before booking. Exam domains and objectives

  • Translate a business requirement into a secure architecture with defined controls and clear trade-offs
  • Integrate security into engineering and development workflows without blocking delivery
  • Analyse risk data and choose treatments that match the organisation's stated risk appetite
  • Design identity, access and cryptographic solutions that scale across cloud and on premises systems
  • Evaluate security operations, monitoring and incident response capabilities and recommend improvements
  • Communicate security decisions and their cost to executives, auditors and technical teams
