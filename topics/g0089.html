G0089 The White Company

G0089Enterprise GroupExtremeThreat Group

New to this? Start here

Explained in plain language

Written for beginners. No security background needed.

Simple definition

The White Company is a name used for criminals who break into banks and trading companies for money. Public reports say the group works for hire, meaning other criminals pay it to do the technical part. It used digital certificates, which are files that make software look trusted, so that harmful programs seemed official. Reports connect the group with activity in the Middle East.

Why should you care?

Software signatures are supposed to prove that a program is safe. When criminals get valid certificates, that trust is misplaced, and your tools may allow the program to run. A group that works for hire also makes tracing harder, because the same tools appear in many unrelated cases. Focus on what a program does rather than only on who signed it. Payment approval checks remain the strongest defence against this kind of theft.

How it works, in plain steps

  1. The group is paid by other criminals to break into a chosen target.
  2. They get an initial foothold through email tricks or an exposed internet service.
  3. They sign their tools with a valid certificate so the files look trusted.
  4. They then install remote access software and learn the target's money systems.
  5. They prepare fraudulent transfers or trades by following the internal procedures exactly.
  6. The money is then moved through bank accounts that their clients control.

Real-world example

Imagine a locksmith who also sells keys to burglars. He carries a real licence, so building managers let him work without questions. The keys he copies are handed to someone else, who returns at night. Checking the licence is not enough. You also need to watch what the locksmith does while he is inside the building.

Key takeaways

  • A trusted software signature does not guarantee that the program is safe.
  • Groups that work for hire make attribution between cases harder.
  • Control over payment approval stops theft even when an intruder is inside.

In more detail

The White Company is a financially motivated group that public reporting describes as offering intrusion services for hire, with its name appearing in connection with high value thefts rather than with espionage. Reporting associates the group with activity in the Middle East and with the targeting of banks, financial institutions and exchanges, using code signing certificates to make harmful software look legitimate and long term access to reach payment and trading systems. The business model matters as much as the tools: this cluster behaves like a contractor, selling or lending capability to others, so the same tooling appears across incidents that are otherwise unrelated. Defenders should therefore expect gaps between published indicators and what they see locally, and should focus on the behaviour of signed binaries, unusual approval activity and remote access from unexpected locations.

What you will practise

  • Explain why attackers seek valid code signing certificates.
  • Describe the risk of intrusion for hire or criminal service models.
  • Explain why behaviour based detection is needed alongside signature checks.
  • List controls that limit losses once an attacker reaches payment systems.
  • Explain how attribution changes when one group serves many clients.
