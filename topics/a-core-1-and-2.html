CompTIA A+ Core 1 & 2 (V15)

In more detail

CompTIA A+ Core 1 & 2 (V15) is a vendor certification offered by CompTIA. This independent study guide covers all 9 objective domains: Mobile devices, Networking, Hardware, Virtualization and cloud computing, Hardware and network troubleshooting, Operating systems, Security, Software troubleshooting, Operational procedures. Each domain is broken into 26 lessons in total, and every lesson has a MEDIUM and a HARD scenario exam. The exams use realistic workplace situations rather than recall questions, so you practise applying each objective the way the job asks for it.
