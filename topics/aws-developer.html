DVA-C02 AWS Certified Developer - Associate (DVA-C02)

DVA-C02AWSVery hardCertificationUnofficial Study Resource

In more detail

AWS Certified Developer - Associate (DVA-C02) is an associate-level certification for practitioners who build and operate on AWS. The exam is organised into 4 content domains: Development with AWS Services (32%), Security (26%), Deployment (24%), Troubleshooting and Optimization (18%). It is delivered as 50 scored questions and 15 unscored, scored against a scaled pass mark of 720 out of 1,000 with no per-domain minimum, so strength in one domain offsets weakness in another. Every task statement in this topic carries a MEDIUM and a HARD exercise batch, so the practice material follows the published outline of the exam rather than a general reading list. The statements here are taken from the exam guide, and the task list is the one AWS publishes: if AWS revises the guide, this topic is revised with it.
