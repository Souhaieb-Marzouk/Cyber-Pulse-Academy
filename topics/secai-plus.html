In more detail

CompTIA SecAI+ is a vendor certification offered by CompTIA. This independent study guide covers all 4 objective domains: Basic AI concepts related to cybersecurity, Securing AI systems, AI-assisted security, AI governance, risk, and compliance. Each domain is broken into 12 lessons in total, and every lesson has a MEDIUM and a HARD scenario exam. The exams use realistic workplace situations rather than recall questions, so you practise applying each objective the way the job asks for it.
