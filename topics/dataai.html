In more detail

CompTIA DataAI is a vendor certification offered by CompTIA. This independent study guide covers all 5 objective domains: Mathematics and statistics, Modeling, analysis, and outcomes, Machine learning, Operations and processes, Specialized applications of data science. Each domain is broken into 25 lessons in total, and every lesson has a MEDIUM and a HARD scenario exam. The exams use realistic workplace situations rather than recall questions, so you practise applying each objective the way the job asks for it.

Unofficial Study Resource. Unofficial Study Resource.
