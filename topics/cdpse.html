Simple definition

The Certified Data Privacy Solutions Engineer (CDPSE) is a certification from ISACA. It is for people who build and check the systems that keep personal data safe. It is not only about writing privacy rules. It is about making privacy work inside real technology.

Why should you care?

Many laws now say that companies must protect personal data. Companies need people who can turn those laws into working controls. This certificate shows an employer that you can do that. It helps you move from a general security role into privacy work. It also proves that you understand both the legal side and the technical side.

How it works, in plain steps

  1. Read the official ISACA exam outline first, so you know the three domains and what each one asks of you.
  2. Study privacy law basics, such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which is the data law of the European Union.
  3. Learn how privacy controls are built into systems, including data mapping and consent records.
  4. Practise with a question bank that explains answers, so you learn the reasoning and not just the fact.
  5. Book the exam when your practice scores are steady, then review weak domains in the last week.
  6. The exam itself is multiple choice, taken at a test centre or online, and it tests judgement more than memory.

Real-world example

Think of a hospital that stores patient records. A doctor needs the full history to treat a patient. A billing clerk needs only the name and the payment details. A privacy engineer looks at that system and asks who can see what, how long each record is kept, and what happens when it is deleted. The engineer then sets rules in the software so the billing clerk simply cannot open the full history. That is the kind of work this certification is about.

Key takeaways

  • CDPSE is aimed at experienced people, so check the work experience requirement before you pay for the exam.
  • You need both legal knowledge and technical skill, so do not skip either side.
  • Learn to read the exam outline closely, because it tells you exactly which domains are tested.

In more detail

The Certified Data Privacy Solutions Engineer (CDPSE) is an ISACA certification for experienced privacy and technology professionals who build privacy into systems rather than only writing policy. It sits at a mid to senior level, aimed at engineers, architects, auditors, and privacy programme staff with several years of relevant work. It proves to an employer that you can design privacy controls, assess data handling risk, and manage retention across the full data lifecycle in line with laws such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The exam uses multiple choice questions covering three domains, and ISACA also requires qualifying work experience. ISACA has announced a revision of this exam, so the domain structure below follows the long established published objectives and omits weightings that cannot be confirmed; check the vendor's current exam content outline before booking. It leads to roles such as privacy engineer, data protection officer support, security architect, and privacy programme manager. This is an independent study guide, not official ISACA material.

Exam domains and objectives

  • Describe how privacy governance fits with an organisation's overall risk and compliance work
  • Explain the main duties that data protection law places on a company that handles personal data
  • Identify where personal data is collected, moved, stored and shared across a business
  • Judge the privacy risk of a new system or a change to an existing one
  • Select technical controls such as encryption, masking and access limits for personal data
  • Plan retention and deletion rules that match legal duties and business needs
