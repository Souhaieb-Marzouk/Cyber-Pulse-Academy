AIB-C01 AWS Certified AI Business Strategist (AIB-C01)

AIB-C01AWSHardCertificationUnofficial Study Resource

In more detail

AWS Certified AI Business Strategist (AIB-C01) is a foundational certification for people starting with AWS who need to speak about it accurately rather than build on it. The exam is organised into 4 content domains: AI Fundamentals and Literacy (24%), AI Strategy and Business Value Creation (28%), AI Governance and Responsible AI Leadership (24%), Business Readiness, Leadership, and AI Transformation (24%). It is delivered as scored questions, scored against a scaled pass mark of 700 out of 1,000 with no per-domain minimum, so strength in one domain offsets weakness in another. Every task statement in this topic carries a MEDIUM and a HARD exercise batch, so the practice material follows the published outline of the exam rather than a general reading list. The statements here are taken from the exam guide, and the task list is the one AWS publishes: if AWS revises the guide, this topic is revised with it.
