In more detail

EC-Council Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery is a professional certification offered by EC-Council. This independent study guide covers all 10 course modules: Foundations of Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery, Business Continuity Management, Risk Assessment, Business Impact Analysis, Business Continuity Plan, Disaster Recovery Planning, Data Backup and System Recovery, Disaster Recovery Plan Development, Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Plan Activation, Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Plan Activation Test, Training. Every module has a MEDIUM and a HARD scenario exam, written as realistic workplace situations rather than recall questions, so you practise applying each module the way the job asks for it.
