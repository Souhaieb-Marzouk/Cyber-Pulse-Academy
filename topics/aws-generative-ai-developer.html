AIP-C01 AWS Certified Generative AI Developer - Professional (AIP-C01)

AIP-C01AWSExtremeCertificationUnofficial Study Resource

In more detail

AWS Certified Generative AI Developer - Professional (AIP-C01) is a professional-level certification for practitioners who design and operate complex AWS estates. The exam is organised into 5 content domains: Foundation Model Integration, Data Management, and Compliance (31%), Implementation and Integration (26%), AI Safety, Security, and Governance (20%), Operational Efficiency and Optimization for GenAI Applications (12%), Testing, Validation, and Troubleshooting (11%). It is delivered as 65 scored questions and 10 unscored, scored against a scaled pass mark of 750 out of 1,000 with no per-domain minimum, so strength in one domain offsets weakness in another. Every task statement in this topic carries a MEDIUM and a HARD exercise batch, so the practice material follows the published outline of the exam rather than a general reading list. The statements here are taken from the exam guide, and the task list is the one AWS publishes: if AWS revises the guide, this topic is revised with it.
