In more detail

CompTIA Ethical Hacker Pro is a vendor certification offered by CompTIA. This independent study guide covers all 1 objective domains: Core domains. Each domain is broken into 6 lessons in total, and every lesson has a MEDIUM and a HARD scenario exam. The exams use realistic workplace situations rather than recall questions, so you practise applying each objective the way the job asks for it.
