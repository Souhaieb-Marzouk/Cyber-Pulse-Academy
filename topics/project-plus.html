CompTIA Project+

CompTIAExtremeCertificationUnofficial Study Resource

In more detail

CompTIA Project+ is a vendor certification offered by CompTIA. This independent study guide covers all 4 objective domains: Project management concepts, Project life cycle phases, Tools and documentation, Basics of IT and governance. Each domain is broken into 24 lessons in total, and every lesson has a MEDIUM and a HARD scenario exam. The exams use realistic workplace situations rather than recall questions, so you practise applying each objective the way the job asks for it.

Unofficial Study Resource. Unofficial Study Resource. Cyber Pulse Academy is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by CompTIA, ISC2, ISACA, OffSec, EC-Council, GIAC, Microsoft, AWS, Google, Cisco, or any other vendor. Objectives summaries are paraphrased from publicly available exam objectives.
