G0122 Silent Librarian

G0122Enterprise GroupExtremeThreat Group

New to this? Start here

Explained in plain language

Written for beginners. No security background needed.

Simple definition

Silent Librarian is a group that steals university logins. It builds fake web pages that look exactly like a school login screen. Then it sends emails that push students and teachers to those pages. The group is also called TA407 and COBALT DICKENS, and reporting links it to Iranian interests.

Why should you care?

One stolen login can open email, research papers and private student records. Universities have many users and open networks, which makes them a soft target. If you reuse the same password everywhere, one fake page can unlock many accounts. This group also shows how a simple trick can beat expensive security tools, because it targets people rather than software.

How it works, in plain steps

  1. The attacker copies a real university login page and hosts it on a domain name that looks almost the same.
  2. A phishing email or message tells the reader to sign in to fix a problem or read a shared document.
  3. The fake page collects the username and password and sends them to the attacker.
  4. The attacker logs in to mail, library portals and journal databases with those real details.
  5. They may add hidden mail rules to forward or delete messages that could reveal the theft.
  6. They repeat the process with many users, because more accounts mean more access.

Real-world example

Picture a hospital night desk. A stranger in a clean uniform stands by the staff entrance and holds out a sign-in sheet that matches the real one. Tired workers sign it without looking twice. The stranger now has a list of names and door codes. Nobody broke a lock. Silent Librarian does the same with a fake login page, and the list it collects is a set of working passwords.

Key takeaways

  • Check the web address before typing a password, because lookalike domains are easy to build.
  • Turn on multi-factor authentication so a stolen password alone is not enough.
  • Review mail rules and forwarding settings if you think an account was taken over.

In more detail

Silent Librarian, also tracked as TA407 and COBALT DICKENS, is a credential phishing operation that public reporting links to Iranian interests and describes as targeting universities, research libraries and academic publishing services worldwide. Rather than exploiting software flaws, the group builds convincing lookalike login pages for single sign-on portals, library systems and scholarly databases, then lures students, faculty and librarians to them with phishing email and messages. Harvested credentials are used to access licensed journals, mailboxes and, in some documented cases, other systems that reuse the same passwords. The activity has been reported across multiple academic years with infrastructure rebuilt after takedowns. Evidence includes lookalike domains registered close in time to a phishing wave, authentication failures followed by successful logins from unexpected locations, and mailbox rules created by an attacker.
