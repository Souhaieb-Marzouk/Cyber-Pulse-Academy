📚 What you will learn here

This EXTREMELY HARD exam puts M1042 where senior people meet it: inside decisions. M1042 — Disable or Remove Feature or Program — is a Mitigation object in MITRE ATT&CK, and at this level you defend how you apply it. The scenarios chain three or more ideas, mix signals that contradict each other, and price every action in downtime, evidence or trust. You will decide whether to act at all, not only which feature to disable. M1042 — Disable or Remove Feature or Program — stays the anchor: the ATT&CK page links it to the techniques behind every scenario, from app passwords to WinRM, from LLMNR to web shells.

What the expert level adds. You will resolve a conflict between a live packet capture and a console that says 'Applied: Yes'; keep two of twelve servers working for the night shift while disabling ten; protect volatile evidence before an uninstall; tell the board why the industry's favourite 'disable all scripting' reflex is wrong for an environment whose automation runs on PowerShell; and choose the compensating control (allowlisting, filtering, MFA) when a feature genuinely must stay. The terminal exercise asks for a reversible configuration command under a business constraint — with a destructive near-miss waiting for the careless.

How to work through it. Read all artefacts first, including the noise. When two signals conflict, decide which one measures reality. When an action has a cost, weigh it openly. When the evidence is incomplete, the correct answer is to gather more of it — 'do nothing yet' is a real decision, not a failure. Partial credit applies everywhere, and one hint per exercise costs ten percent.
