📚 What you will learn here

This HARD exam treats M1042 the way a senior practitioner meets it: through discrimination. M1042 — Disable or Remove Feature or Program — is a Mitigation object in MITRE ATT&CK: a defensive control that switches off what attacker techniques depend on. You already know that M1042 — Disable or Remove Feature or Program — removes the unnecessary software, features and services that attacker techniques depend on. Here the scenarios are longer, the artefacts come in sets, and at least two answer options will look defensible. Your job is to find the one that is defensible in this world, with this ticket, this log and this constraint.

What the HARD level adds. You will correlate a procedure with the exact ATT&CK row it belongs to (proxy binaries, adversary-in-the-middle, session hijacking), scope removals so that the business keeps working, sequence containment so evidence is not destroyed, and catch the traps: a change that looks alarming at 03:12 but is the hardening window doing its job; a control that looks applied in the console but fails on a legacy host; a 'best practice' removal that would break the admins who keep the company running. The M1042 page itself warns about these: PowerShell needs an impact review, MMC is an admin tool, CMSTP may be needed for VPN profile installs, and disabling auto-mount does not deactivate the mount functionality itself.

One exercise is a terminal task. You will type a real Windows command into a simulated shell to disable an optional feature. Nothing executes — it is a simulation, graded element by element, with partial credit.

Read every artefact before answering. The obvious answer is sometimes planted.
