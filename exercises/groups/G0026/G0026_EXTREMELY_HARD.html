What you will learn here

This is the top tier of the G0026 APT18 series. The page's rows are your vocabulary; here you must think in sentences and chains: multi-artefact cases with noise, clocks that disagree, change tickets that explain too much, and stakeholders who demand verdicts in minutes.

APT18's profile becomes a live playbook under pressure: valid credentials entering external remote services while MFA is absent, Run-key persistence returning on hosts, at.exe staging with its cleanup, two C2 channels (HTTP and DNS) that take turns when you block one, tooling that is commodity (gh0st RAT) and therefore never a complete attribution, and deletion behaviour that legal will ask about word for word. Around every row you will meet the senior analyst's real opponents: conflicting signals, incomplete evidence, costly levers, and widely recommended best practices that are the wrong answer for this case.

You will act in the seats where these calls live: the analyst who must hold a verdict before a CISO bridge, the responder managing five decisions on a finance host under legal constraints, the detection engineer finding the one mis-mapped row, the CTI lead wording claims that executives quote verbatim, the voice on the 03:00 regulator call. Several correct answers here are the disciplined ones - do nothing yet, buy information - and one lever costs the business so much that the evidence does not justify it.

Every artefact stays grounded in the G0026 page and the references it cites. Expect long scenarios built from labelled artefact panels, irrelevant-but-realistic noise, and at least one trap per exercise. Read the audit notes first; they are where the clocks and the owners hide.

How this exam works

  • Passing score: you pass with 70% or more, counted only over answered exercises.
  • 17 exercises, one per screen. Types in this file: verdict calls, conflict-resolution multi-selects, a five-step branching case, coverage analysis, a skewed-clock log viewer, pattern anchoring, priority ranking, mapping review, a six-blank brief, triage with three verdicts, and single-choice pressure calls.
  • Timer: a 55:00 countdown. Pause stops it and hides the exercise. Check stops it automatically while feedback is open and resumes on Next. In the branching case only the final Check pauses; intermediate step confirmations keep the timer running. Skipping never stops the timer.
  • Partial credit: multi-item exercises award (H − W) / N - wrong selections subtract; selecting everything scores zero.
  • Hints: one per exercise, costing 10% of that exercise's credit. At this tier hints restate a constraint you may have missed; they never point at answers.
  • Skips: a review screen before the end lets you answer skipped exercises until the timer ends; never-answered exercises are excluded from the score.
