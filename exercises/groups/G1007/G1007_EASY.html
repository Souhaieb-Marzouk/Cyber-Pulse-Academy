How this exam works

  • Passing score: you need at least 70% to pass. Your live score is shown in the top bar at all times.
  • Exercise types: scenario triage, multiple choice, multi-select, drag-and-drop, matching, ordering, fill-in-the-blank, log analysis and short branching cases. Each exercise shows a SCENARIO box, a QUESTION box and a YOUR ANSWER box.
  • Timer: a countdown runs while you work. The Pause button stops it for a real break and hides the exercise until you press Resume.
  • Check buys you time: when you press Check, the timer stops by itself so you can read the feedback with no pressure. The timer starts again only when you press Next. The header shows ⏸ PAUSED — reviewing feedback (resumes on Next) while this state is active.
  • Partial credit: exercises with several correct items give you credit for each item you get right. Wrong selections subtract from correct ones (the formula gives credit max(0, (H − W) / N)). Selecting everything "to be safe" gives zero. A partially correct answer still earns its fractional credit and is labelled PARTIALLY CORRECT.
  • Hint: you may use one hint per exercise. A hint costs −10% of that exercise's credit (never 10% of the whole exam).
  • Skip and review: the Skip button moves an exercise to a "skipped" pool without revealing the answer. Skipping never stops the timer and costs nothing. Before you finish, a review screen lets you return to skipped exercises as many times as you want, as long as the timer has not ended. Skipped exercises you never answer are excluded from your score (they count neither for you nor against you).
  • Retake: if you fail, you can retake the exam. Questions and answer choices are shuffled again.
