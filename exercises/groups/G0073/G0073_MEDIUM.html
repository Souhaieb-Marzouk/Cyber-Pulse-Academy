What you will learn here

You already know the basics: G0073 is APT19, a China-based espionage group also tracked as Codoso, C0d0so0, Codoso Team, or Sunshop Group. At this level you connect two ideas at a time. You will link the group's campaigns to its delivery tricks: the 2017 phishing campaign that hit seven law and investment firms, and older public reporting of a watering-hole attack on the popular Forbes.com website. You will also connect its tools — Cobalt Strike (S0154) and Empire (S0363) — to the noise they leave in logs, and to the controls that blunt them.

The work here mirrors real jobs. A detection engineer turns beacon behaviour into a SIEM rule and picks the right data sources (process logs, network traffic, account logins). A SOC analyst triages alerts where a benign event and a malicious event look almost the same. A CTI analyst writes attribution carefully — for example, some analysts track APT19 and Deep Panda as one group, but open sources do not settle the question, so a good report states a confidence level instead of a fact. A GRC analyst maps controls like MFA (M1032), user training (M1017), and endpoint behaviour prevention (M1040) onto the group's playbook.

Every exercise makes you take two steps: decide what matters first, then connect it to the second fact. Read the feedback after each Check — it explains both steps.

How this exam works

  • Pass mark: you need 70% or more to pass. Exercises you answer are each worth up to 1 point.
  • Exercise types in this file: single-choice questions, drag-and-drop sorting exercises, matching exercises, SOC alert-queue triage simulations, fill-in-the-blank exercises, spot-the-mistake exercises, timeline ordering exercises, multi-select questions with partial credit.
  • Timer: a countdown of 40 minutes starts when you press Start. The timer stops the moment you press Check, so you can read the feedback and the explanation for as long as you like. It starts again when you press Next (or Final Submit on the last exercise). The Pause button stops the timer and hides the exercise for a real break; press Resume to continue.
  • Partial credit: exercises with several correct items are scored fairly, item by item: credit = max(0, (correct items − wrong items) ÷ total items). Selecting everything does not help, because wrong items subtract from your score. The live score in the header shows your running total.
  • Hints: every exercise has one hint. A hint gives you a nudge, never the answer, and costs 10% of that exercise's credit (one hint per exercise).
  • Skips: every exercise has a Skip button. Skipped exercises are moved to a review pool without showing the answer. Before the results you can return to the pool and answer them, as many times as you want, until the timer ends or you submit. Skipped exercises that stay unanswered are excluded from your score completely.
  • Press Start when you are ready. Good luck!
