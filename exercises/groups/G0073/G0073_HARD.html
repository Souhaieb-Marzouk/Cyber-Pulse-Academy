What you will learn here

You know the group's profile: APT19, a China-based espionage group also called Codoso, C0d0so0, Codoso Team, or Sunshop Group; the 2017 phishing campaign against seven law and investment firms; the Forbes.com watering-hole history; Cobalt Strike (S0154) and Empire (S0363) as its listed software. At this level the answers stop being obvious. Every exercise puts two defensible options side by side and asks which one survives this scenario — because a shortcut that works elsewhere fails here.

The situations mirror hard real work. A SOC analyst triages a law-firm estate where a paralegal's travel and an operator's persistence look similar on one dashboard. A detection engineer writes beacon queries that must survive jitter and domain fronting, and audits rules where one wrong predicate buries the signal. An incident responder sequences actions under legal hold, where "best practice" done at the wrong moment destroys evidence or tips the operator. A CTI analyst defends an attribution line against a partner who wants certainty the evidence cannot give — the Deep Panda overlap stays open, and the report must say so.

Expect near-misses: options that are technically defensible in a different context but wrong here. Expect at least one case where the alarming thing is benign, one where the calm thing is dangerous, and one where the correct action is the least aggressive one. Hints remove one wrong path — they never name the answer.

How this exam works

  • Pass mark: you need 70% or more to pass. Exercises you answer are each worth up to 1 point.
  • Exercise types in this file: SOC alert-queue triage simulations, single-choice questions, spot-the-mistake exercises, drag-and-drop sorting exercises, multi-select questions with partial credit, timeline ordering exercises, fill-in-the-blank exercises, matching exercises, prioritization / ranking exercises.
  • Timer: a countdown of 55 minutes starts when you press Start. The timer stops the moment you press Check, so you can read the feedback and the explanation for as long as you like. It starts again when you press Next (or Final Submit on the last exercise). The Pause button stops the timer and hides the exercise for a real break; press Resume to continue.
  • Partial credit: exercises with several correct items are scored fairly, item by item: credit = max(0, (correct items − wrong items) ÷ total items). Selecting everything does not help, because wrong items subtract from your score. The live score in the header shows your running total.
  • Hints: every exercise has one hint. A hint gives you a nudge, never the answer, and costs 10% of that exercise's credit (one hint per exercise).
  • Skips: every exercise has a Skip button. Skipped exercises are moved to a review pool without showing the answer. Before the results you can return to the pool and answer them, as many times as you want, until the timer ends or you submit. Skipped exercises that stay unanswered are excluded from your score completely.
  • Press Start when you are ready. Good luck!
