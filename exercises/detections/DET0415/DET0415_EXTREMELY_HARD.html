What you will learn here

This is the top tier, and it treats you like the senior engineer on call. DET0415 watches T1499.003 — Application Exhaustion Flood across four platforms, but the analytics never announce the truth: legitimate surges, deployments, scanners and scheduled jobs all imitate parts of the flood signature, and incomplete evidence is the norm.

Every exercise here chains three or more concepts and gives you at least one conflicting signal you must resolve explicitly. Several correct answers are to gather more evidence or escalate rather than act; some correct actions carry a real cost you must accept and name. One widely-cited best practice is, in these facts, the wrong answer.

You will also meet the tools of the trade at senior level: a read-only query editor against a cloud log store where the query is the easy part and the evidence discipline is the test; tuning that must survive bursts that are business-normal; a source-table attestation with a planted error; and an executive summary whose every word is a commitment.

Who does this work? A senior SOC analyst holds the pager, a detection engineer owns the tuning PRs, an incident commander weighs containment costs, a cloud engineer reads CloudWatch and CloudTrail under pressure, and a CTI lead briefs the CISO with exact vocabulary. Take your time inside each exercise: the timer paces the exam, but after every Check the clock stops and the explanation is yours to study.
