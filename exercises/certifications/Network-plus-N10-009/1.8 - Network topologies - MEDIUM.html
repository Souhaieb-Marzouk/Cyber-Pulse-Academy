CompTIA Network+ (N10-009) · Objective 1.8

Network topologies — practice exam

You are the on-call network engineer. This exam drops you into realistic work — WAN rollouts, data-center fabrics, campus upgrades, live switch logs, change windows — and asks you to make the calls a real engineer makes. It drills the seven topologies of objective 1.8: mesh, hybrid, star / hub-and-spoke, spine and leaf, point-to-point, three-tier, and collapsed core, plus the design trade-offs around them.

Keywords covered

Exercise types you will meet

  • Decision walk-throughs — pick each step of a live incident or design call and see the consequence immediately.
  • Sort into buckets & match pairs — drag with the mouse, or tap an item and then its target (works on touch screens).
  • Order & rank — rebuild a timeline or rank designs, with arrow buttons or dragging.
  • Fill the blanks — complete a real design memo from a shuffled word bank.
  • Log review — a filter box, color-coded log lines, and questions about what the logs actually say.
  • Terminal — type the actual command into a fake device shell and see its output.
  • Find the faulty line — click the broken line in a design document before it becomes an outage.
  • Multiple choice & multi-select — used sparingly, with near-miss distractors.

How answering works

  • Check validates your answer, marks each element green (correct) or red (wrong), shows the explanation, and pauses the timer in review mode while everything stays visible. The clock restarts only when you press Next.
  • Revise (after a check) clears the exercise so you can try again. The old score stands until you Check again, and a new Check re-applies the review pause.
  • Skip jumps ahead and flags the exercise (amber in the navigator). You can return to skipped exercises any time before the timer ends. At submit, anything never checked is excluded from scoring — removed from both your points and the maximum.
  • Partial credit: multi-part exercises award points per correct element. A wrong pick earns nothing extra but never subtracts.
  • Hint (−10%): one nudge per exercise; using it costs 10% of that exercise's points. A hint never pauses the timer.

The timer and its two pauses

  • Manual Pause freezes the clock but hides the exercise until you resume.
  • Check-pause (review mode) freezes the clock but keeps everything visible so you can learn from the feedback. Only Next restarts the clock.
  • The two pauses stack: the clock runs again only when both are cleared. If time runs out, the exam submits itself.
