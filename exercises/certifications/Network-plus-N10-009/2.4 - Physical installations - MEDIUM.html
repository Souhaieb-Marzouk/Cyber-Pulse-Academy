How this exam works

  • Goal. Realistic scenario training for this chapter. Every exercise mirrors real on-the-job work (racks, power, environment), not textbook recall.
  • Exercise types. Scenario queues, drag & drop, matching, timeline ordering, ranking, fill-in-the-blank, log and log-viewer analysis, branching decisions, dual-perspective items, a terminal emulator, spot-the-mistake, and a small number of multiple-choice questions.
  • Passing score. — shown live while you answer. Partial credit is given per element on multi-answer exercises (one wrong pick does not erase your correct ones).
  • Check. Press Check to validate: each answer element is marked individually (green = correct, red = wrong, amber = missed/partial). Checking also pauses the timer in review mode: the exercise, the marks and the explanation stay fully visible. The timer resumes only when you press Next.
  • Skip. Skip jumps to the next exercise without scoring it. Skipped items show an amber marker. Until time runs out you can navigate back and finish them; anything still skipped at submit time is excluded from both score and total.
  • Revise. After a Check you may press Revise to rework the exercise, then Check again. Your last Check is what counts.
  • Two pauses. Manual Pause hides the exercise so you cannot study it while the clock is frozen. Check-pause keeps everything visible for review. They stack independently; the timer runs only when both are cleared.
  • Hints. One hint per exercise reveals a nudge (never the answer) at a cost of −10% of that exercise’s points.
