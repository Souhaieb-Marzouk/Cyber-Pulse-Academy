ABOUT THIS EXAM

This is a realistic practice exam for HARD severity learners. It covers one chapter only: 3.8 — Access and management (VPNs, SSH, GUI, API, console). Every exercise is built from real work: device configs, logs, tickets, SOC queues and terminals. You will manage real-feeling incidents, not textbook trivia.

You need 70% to pass. The clock runs for 36 minutes (about 72 seconds per exercise — the HARD pace is tighter because expert-level recall is expected). Partial credit is real: multi-answer exercises pay you for each correct element.

HOW IT WORKS

Check — press it to score the current exercise. You see which parts were right (green ✓), wrong (red ✗) or missed (amber ⚠), plus an explanation. After a Check the timer pauses for review: content stays visible, an indicator shows “Timer paused — review mode”, and only Next restarts the clock.

Manual Pause — the ⏸ button hides the exercise (blur) so you cannot study while paused. Press again to resume. The two pause types stack: the clock only runs when both are cleared.

Skip & return — Skip jumps to the next exercise and marks it amber in the navigator. Until you submit (or time runs out), you can click any number in the navigator to go back and finish skipped items. Skipped-and-unanswered exercises are excluded from scoring: removed from both your score and the total.

Revise — after a Check you may unlock an exercise and answer again; it is only scored when you press Check again.

Hints — up to 2 per exercise, each costs −10% of that exercise’s points. A hint nudges; it never gives the answer.

Partial credit — multi-answer exercises (multi-select, drag & drop, matching, ordering, fills, terminals, multi-step scenarios) earn a share of the point per correct element. Wrong picks earn nothing extra but do not subtract. Single-answer exercises are all-or-nothing.
