Scenario-based exam on SNMP, flow data, packet capture, baseline metrics, log aggregation, API integration and port mirroring. Every exercise asks you to reason like a real NOC/SOC engineer — not to recall definitions.

30 exercises ⏱ 55 minutes Pass mark: 70% Severity: MEDIUM Partial credit on multi-part items

How this exam works

  • Three blocks per exercise: SCENARIO (the setup) → QUESTION (what you must decide) → ANSWERS (how to answer). The question is never hidden inside the logs.
  • Check validates and locks your answer, marks each part green (✓ correct) or red (✗ wrong), shows the explanation, and pauses the timer in review mode. The exercise stays fully visible so you can learn. Only pressing Next resumes the clock.
  • Manual Pause hides/blurs the exercise and freezes the clock. Resume brings it back. The two pause reasons stack; the clock only runs when both are cleared.
  • Skip jumps to the next exercise (amber ⚠ chip). You can navigate back to skipped or answered items until the timer ends. Skipped items are excluded from scoring — out of both your points and the total.
  • Partial credit: multi-answer items (multi-select, matching, ordering, fill-in, branching, spot-the-line) award points per correct element. A wrong pick earns nothing for that element but never subtracts.
  • Hint reveals a nudge, never the answer, and costs −1 pt (10% of the exercise). One hint per exercise. Press Revise to unlock a checked answer and change it.
  • At the end you get a full report: your answer, the correct answer, points earned, and an explanation for every item — including skipped ones. Retake reshuffles everything.
  • All addresses and hostnames are fictional training data (defanged, e.g. 10[.]0[.]0[.]5).

Exercise types you will meet

  • Single-choice and multi-select questions with tricky distractors
  • Click-to-place matching boards (terms → meanings)
  • Ordering rows (▲▼) — triage, severity, workflow
  • Fill-in-the-blank configs and runbooks from a shuffled word bank
  • A fake terminal where you type the actual command
  • Branching scenarios — each choice changes what happens next
  • Spot-the-broken-line config reviews
  • A log viewer with a filter box for log analysis items
