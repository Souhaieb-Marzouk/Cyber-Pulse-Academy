What this exam is

A certification-style practice exam for CompTIA Network+ (N10-009), objective 5.3 — Network services issues: switching issues (STP, VLAN assignment, ACLs), routing issues (routing table and default routes), address pool exhaustion, and incorrect gateway / IP / subnet mask. Every exercise is a realistic work scenario, not a textbook quiz.

How it works

  • Check — validates your answer inline (green = correct, red = wrong, amber = partial / missed), locks it for scoring, and pauses the timer in review mode. Content and explanation stay fully visible. The timer resumes only when you press Next.
  • Manual Pause — a separate button. It hides/blurs the exercise while the clock is frozen. The two pause reasons stack: the clock only runs when both are cleared.
  • Skip — jump ahead. Skipped exercises get an amber marker and are excluded from scoring (removed from both your points and the maximum). You can navigate back to any skipped exercise and finish it while the timer still runs.
  • Partial credit — multi-answer exercises (multi-select, drag & drop, matching, ordering, blanks, branching) are scored per correct element. A wrong extra selection never subtracts points; it simply earns nothing.
  • Hint — one per exercise, reveals a nudge (never the answer) and costs 10% of that exercise's points.
  • Revise — after a Check, unlock your answer, change it, and press Check again to re-validate (the review pause applies again).
  • Retake — questions and answer choices are reshuffled; timer, skips, hints and partial scores are all reset.

Exercise types you will meet

Single-choice and multi-select, click-the-faulty-line, timeline ordering, matching, bucket sorting, fill-in-the-blank from a shuffled word bank, a log viewer with a live filter box, an interactive fake terminal, and branching incident walk-throughs. All keyboard-accessible; dark mode; reduced-motion respected.
