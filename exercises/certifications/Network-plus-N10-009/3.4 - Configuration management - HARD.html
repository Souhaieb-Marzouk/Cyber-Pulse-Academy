Keywords covered

How this exam works

  • 30 exercises on objective 3.4 (configuration management: production, backup and baseline configurations, plus the documentation and recovery topics around them). Pass mark: 70%.
  • Exercise types: scenario choices, multi-select, drag & drop, matching, ordering and ranking, fill-in-the-blank, clickable "find the mistake" lines, a log viewer with a filter box, a fake terminal, branching incidents and triage tables.
  • Check validates your answer, marks each element green/red/amber, shows the explanation, and pauses the timer in review mode. The clock restarts only when you press Next.
  • Pause (manual) hides the exercise behind a blur and stops the clock. Press Resume to continue. The two pauses are independent and can stack.
  • Skip moves on and marks the exercise "not counted". You can return to any exercise until you submit or time runs out. Skipped exercises are removed from both the points you earn and the points possible.
  • Multi-answer exercises give partial credit per correct element. Hint reveals a nudge and costs 10% of that exercise's points (one hint per exercise).
  • On retake, questions and answer choices are reshuffled.
