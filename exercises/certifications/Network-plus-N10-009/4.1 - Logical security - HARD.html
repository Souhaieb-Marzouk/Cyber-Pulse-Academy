How this exam works

Thirty HARD-severity exercises for Objective 4.1 — Logical security. Expect subtle distractors, near-miss answers, ambiguous artefacts and reasoning across several sources at once. The obvious answer is sometimes wrong; read every artefact before deciding. One exercise is an unguided terminal: only the operational goal is given, you pick the tool and flags.

Rules you must know before you start

  • Check grades your answer, shows green/red/amber marks and the explanation, and pauses the timer in review mode. The exercise stays fully visible so you can learn from it. The timer resumes only when you press Next.
  • Pause (manual) hides and blurs the exercise while the clock is stopped. Press it again to resume. The two pauses can stack — the clock runs again only when both are cleared.
  • Skip jumps to the next exercise and flags this one as skipped. Skipped exercises are excluded from scoring (neither earned points nor possible points). You can come back to them until the timer ends or you submit.
  • Partial credit: multi-answer exercises (multi-select, drag & drop, matching, ordering, multi-blank) award points per correct element, not all-or-nothing.
  • Hints: each hint used costs 10% of that exercise's points. A hint nudges you; it never gives the answer.
  • Revise unlocks a checked exercise so you can change the answer and Check again. Your previous score stands until you re-check.
  • On a retake, questions and answer choices are shuffled again.
