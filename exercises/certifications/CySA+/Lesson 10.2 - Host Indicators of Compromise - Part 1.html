How scoring, hints and the timer work

Every exercise has a point value based on its difficulty. Drag-and-drop, matching, ordering, log analysis and branching scenarios give partial credit for the parts you get right. A wrong answer gives zero for that exercise but never removes points you already earned.

Hint cost: each hint removes 10% of that exercise's points, even if you answer correctly. A hint is a nudge, not the answer. You can use one hint per exercise.

Timer: when the clock reaches zero the exam submits itself. Unanswered exercises score zero. Pausing the clock is allowed, but while paused the question is hidden, so you cannot read it and answer slowly. The limit is weighted per exercise: harder exercises carry more seconds than simple checks.

All indicators of compromise here are defanged, for example 10[.]20[.]30[.]40 and hxxps://cdn-update[.]example. That is a professional habit: it stops a document from becoming a clickable link to real infrastructure.
