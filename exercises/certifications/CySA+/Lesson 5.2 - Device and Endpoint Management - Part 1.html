Your shift

You are L2 endpoint security analyst at Meridian Health Group: 4,800 staff, 6,100 managed devices, 3 hospitals, 2 data centres and a fleet of clinical IoT. You work from the endpoint console (Intune + Defender for Endpoint) and the SOC queue in Splunk. Tickets arrive as INC00xxxxx. Every task below is something this team really does: provisioning, hardening, triage, containment, forensics, de-provisioning and audit response.
