Keywords covered by this exam (extracted from the lesson)

The definitions an auditor or an interviewer will ask for
Incident responseThe organized, methodical process of detecting, analysing, containing, eradicating and recovering from cybersecurity incidents, to minimize damage and restore normal operations.
Security operations center (SOC)The team and the tooling that monitor the estate 24×7, triage the correlated alert queue, and run the incident-response process through Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3.
Event, vulnerability, incidentAn event is something observable; a vulnerability is a weakness that could be used; an incident is a confirmed event that harms, or could harm, the organisation.
Containment vs eradicationContainment limits the damage and stops the spread (isolate, block). Eradication removes the attacker's tools and closes the entry point (delete, rebuild, patch, reset credentials).
Evidence integrityProof that the evidence was not changed: hash before and after acquisition, use a write blocker, and record the chain of custody from collection to storage.
Order of volatilityCollect what disappears first: CPU registers and cache, RAM, live network state, temporary files and pagefile, then disk, then remote logs and archives.
IOC vs IOA vs TTPAn indicator of compromise is a static artefact (hash, IP, domain, registry key). An indicator of attack is behaviour in progress (Office spawning PowerShell). A TTP is the tactic, technique and procedure behind it.
MTTD and MTTRMean time to detect (first attacker action to alert) and mean time to respond or recover (alert to containment and normal service). They are the two key performance indicators of a SOC.
Playbook vs runbookA playbook says what to do and in which order; a runbook gives the technical steps for one task. The playbook is the process, the runbook is the hands.
Containment of the incident vs business continuityContainment protects the systems and the data; the business continuity plan keeps the service running. Both plans must name who may authorise an emergency action.
