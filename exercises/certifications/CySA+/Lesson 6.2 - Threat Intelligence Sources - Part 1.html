TI-500: Threat Intelligence Sources

A hands-on certification exam set inside a real Security Operations Centre. You are the new threat-intelligence analyst on the 07:00 shift. Every task is something a real analyst does: triage feeds, read logs, query a terminal, rank risk, handle sharing rules and write short reports.

Exam mechanics

  • 26 exercises across 5 knowledge areas.
  • Passing score: 80% of all available points.
  • Timer: 35 minutes, running from the moment you start.
  • Pause stops the clock and blurs the exam so you cannot read it while paused.
  • Hint = -10% of that exercise's points. It gives a nudge, not the answer.
  • Every answer shows a full explanation, and the final screen gives a per-question report.
  • If you fail, use Retake Exam: questions and answer choices are shuffled again.

Exercise types you will meet

  • Alert-queue triage and branching incident decisions
  • Drag and drop: classify, sort and map
  • Timeline and severity ranking (drag to reorder)
  • Matching: sources, standards and purposes
  • Log and alert analysis with a live filter box
  • Shell emulator where you type the real command
  • Fill-in-the-blank report and policy drafting
  • Spot-the-mistake configuration review
  • Dual perspective: same event from blue team and red team
  • Multi-select and single-choice with near-miss distractors
