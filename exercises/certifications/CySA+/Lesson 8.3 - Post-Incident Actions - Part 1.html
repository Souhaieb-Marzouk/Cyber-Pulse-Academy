Mission Briefing

Welcome. This is a hands-on certification exam about the post-incident phase of the incident response lifecycle, as described in NIST SP 800-61. You will not answer textbook trivia. You will work a SOC shift, rebuild timelines, triage logs, tune detections, and finish compliance paperwork.

Rules of the exam

• 80% is the passing score.
• 26 exercises, mixed interactive types.
• Each exercise is scored 0–100%. Partial credit exists on multi-part tasks.
• Each exercise has one hint. A hint costs −10% on that exercise only.
• The timer can be paused. While paused the question is hidden, so you cannot read it.

Exercise types you will face

SOC scenario simulations · branching decisions · log and PCAP viewers with filters · SOC alert-queue triage · terminal command entry · drag & drop classification · timeline and priority ordering · matching · policy and report keyword insertion · spot-the-mistake · dual red/blue perspective · multi-select. Only 5 questions are classic multiple choice.
