You will triage a live SOC queue, read real logs, write commands, map MITRE techniques, rank risk and finish a policy and a report. Every item is designed to be hard, and every answer gives a clear, plain-language explanation.

How to work

  • One question at a time. Pick or build your answer, then press Submit answer. The explanation appears straight away.
  • Read the outcome. In scenarios you will see what your choice causes. Use it to learn.
  • Use a hint if you are stuck. It costs 10% of that question, so use it when it helps.
  • Pause when you need a break. The question is hidden and the clock stops, so pausing cannot help you cheat.
  • All indicators of compromise are defanged (for example hxxp and 10[.]0[.]0[.]1) so nothing here looks like live malware infrastructure.
  • Finish the exam to see your score, a per-theme breakdown, a full report and your certificate if you pass.
