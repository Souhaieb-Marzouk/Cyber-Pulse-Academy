How this exam works

  • Live scoring. Each exercise is worth 10 points (230 total). Multi-part exercises give partial credit. Your score and per-theme progress update live in the top bar.
  • Timer. The clock runs while you work. You may pause it — but while paused the exercise is blurred and locked, so pausing cannot help you answer.
  • Hints cost. One hint per exercise for −10% of that exercise's points. It is a nudge, never the answer.
  • No going back. Every answer is graded immediately with a full explanation, then the exam moves on. On a retake, questions and answer choices are reshuffled.
  • Safe IOCs. All hostile indicators are defanged, a professional habit: hxxp://, 10[.]0[.]0[.]1, evil[.]example.
