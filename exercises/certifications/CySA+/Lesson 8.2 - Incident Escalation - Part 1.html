Exam rules

  • Passing score is 80% (200 of 250 points). Multi-part exercises give partial credit.
  • Every exercise gives a hint button. Each hint costs 10% of that exercise score. A hint is a nudge, never the answer.
  • The timer runs during the exam. Press Pause for a break: the exercise is blurred and hidden, so you cannot read it while paused.
  • Submit an exercise to lock it. You then see the full explanation for right and wrong choices.
  • At the end you get a complete report per exercise, with focus tips for what to review. Failing gives a Retake button that reshuffles everything.
