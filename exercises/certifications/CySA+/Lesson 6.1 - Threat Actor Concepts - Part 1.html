How this exam works

  • Every exercise is a realistic work task: SOC alerts, logs, configs, incident steps, policies.
  • One question at a time. Submit it, read the explanation, then move on.
  • A hint reveals a nudge (never the answer) and costs 10% of that question's score.
  • The timer keeps running. Use Pause for a break: the exercise is hidden while paused.
  • At the end you get a full report: what was right, what was wrong, and what to study.

Exercise types you will meet

SOC alert triage • drag & drop categorisation • timeline / risk ordering • log & DNS analysis with a filter box • branching incident decisions • matching • fill-in-the-blank reports and configs • terminal command entry • "spot the mistake" • red vs blue dual perspective • multi-select • single-choice with near-miss distractors.
