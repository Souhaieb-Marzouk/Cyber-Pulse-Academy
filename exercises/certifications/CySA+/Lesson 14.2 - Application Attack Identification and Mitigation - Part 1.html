How this exam works

Exercise types you will meet

  • SOC triage & branching scenarios — your choice changes the next step.
  • Drag & drop — sort controls, attacks and threats into the right group. Tap to select on touch screens.
  • SOC alert queue triage — sort overnight alerts into escalate, investigate or close.
  • Log analysis — read web and auth logs and decide what is happening.
  • Timeline ordering — put response and lifecycle steps in order.
  • Matching — link an attack to the control that really fixes it.
  • Fill in the blank — complete a policy or query with the right word.
  • Spot the mistake — click the broken line in real code or config.
  • Terminal emulator — type the real command in a fake shell.
  • Splunk-style log viewer — filter noise, then answer.
  • Risk ranking — order findings from most to least urgent.
  • Red vs blue — the same bug seen from both sides.

Rules of the shift

  • Each task is worth 10 points. Some tasks give partial credit, so a near-miss still earns part of the score.
  • A Hint button reveals a nudge and costs 10% of that task's points. You can use one hint per task.
  • You must Submit each answer. You cannot change it after you submit.
  • Pause stops the clock, but it blurs and locks the current task so you cannot read it while paused.
  • When the clock reaches zero, the exam ends and anything unanswered scores zero.
  • After the exam you get a full report with an explanation for every task and focus tips for the wrong ones.
  • You can retake the exam. Questions and answer choices are shuffled each time.
Good habit: all indicators in this exam are defanged. You will see hxxp:// instead of a live link and 10[.]0[.]0[.]1 instead of a raw address.
