Attack Surface Management
Certified Analyst Exam

A realistic, scenario-based certification exam on attack surface management. No textbook quizzes — you will triage a live SOC alert, read an adversary's scan logs, fix a broken cloud configuration, rank real risks, and write the monthly security report.

How the exam works

  • 10 exercises · 46 points. Every exercise gives instant feedback with a plain-English explanation.
  • Passing score: 80% — you need 37 of 46 points.
  • Timer: 40 minutes. The clock runs while you work.
  • Pause: the timer stops, but the exercise is hidden while paused — you cannot read it during a break.
  • Retake: everything reshuffles — answers, word banks, and drag items are re-randomized.

Exercise types

  • Branching SOC alert simulation — triage a live alert in 3 steps
  • Drag & drop — lifecycle ordering, finding classification, risk ranking
  • Log analysis — an adversary is scanning your web server
  • Matching — the six core ASM terms
  • Report writing — complete the monthly attack surface review
  • Spot the mistake — a broken Terraform configuration
  • Config completion — the ASM scanner pipeline (YAML)
  • Hard multiple choice, including multi-select
