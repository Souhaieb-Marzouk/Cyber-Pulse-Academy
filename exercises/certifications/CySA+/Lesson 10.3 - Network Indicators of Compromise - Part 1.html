Exam rules and exercise types

Rules of the exam

  • 25 exercises, 100 points (4 points each).
  • Passing score: 80%. Fail means you must retake it.
  • A countdown timer runs. You can pause it, but the question is hidden while paused.
  • A hint costs -10% of that exercise. The hint is a nudge, not the answer.
  • Many exercises give partial credit (drag and drop, ordering, fill in the blanks, branching).
  • At the end you get a full report: what you got right, what you got wrong, and what to study.

Exercise types you will see

  • SOC alert triage and branching incident scenarios
  • Drag and drop: indicators onto categories, techniques onto MITRE ATT&CK tactics
  • Log viewer (filter box) and NetFlow analysis
  • Terminal emulator: type real commands
  • Ordering: response steps, chain of custody, Pyramid of Pain
  • Matching: tools to purpose, attacks to mitigations, frameworks
  • Fill in the blanks: policy, SPL query, sinkhole config, incident report
  • Spot the mistake in a firewall ruleset
  • Dual perspective: blue team vs red team
  • A few multiple-choice questions with tricky distractors
Tip: read every log line. The wrong answer is often the "obvious" one.
