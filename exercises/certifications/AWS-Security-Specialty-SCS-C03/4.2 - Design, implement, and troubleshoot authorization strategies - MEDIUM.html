4.2 — Design, implement, and troubleshoot authorization strategies

MEDIUM severity practice exam for objective 4.2 — realistic scenarios with guided reasoning: alert triage, trust policies and external IDs, ABAC tags, permission boundaries, the Policy Simulator, Access Analyzer findings and remediation workflows.

HOW THIS EXAM WORKS
  • 30 scenario-based exercises, 10 points each. Pass mark: 70%. Timer: 65 minutes total. The timer pauses in two different ways: the Pause button (content is blurred while paused) and the automatic Check-pause (after every Check the clock freezes for review — content stays visible — and only Next restarts it).
  • Check validates, marks every element green/red/amber, locks the answer and shows the explanation. Revise (available after a Check) toggles that explanation back into view — scoring stays locked and the clock stays frozen until you press Next.
  • Skip jumps ahead and marks the exercise in amber. You can return to any skipped or answered exercise until the timer ends. Skipped exercises are excluded from scoring (out of the answered ones only).
  • Partial credit: multi-answer exercises score per correct element. Hint reveals a nudge for −10% of the exercise points.
  • Exercise types: SOC alert triage, drag-and-drop, matching, policy fill-in-the-blank, log viewer with filter, ordering/ranking, branching decisions, terminal commands, multi-select and single-choice questions.
