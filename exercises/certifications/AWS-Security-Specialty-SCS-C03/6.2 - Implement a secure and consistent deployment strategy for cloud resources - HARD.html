How this exam works

  • Check validates your answer, marks every element green/red/amber, locks it for scoring and pauses the timer in review mode so you can read the explanation. Press Next to resume.
  • Skip moves on and flags the exercise amber. You can return to skipped exercises until the timer ends. Skipped exercises are excluded from both score and total.
  • Manual Pause freezes the timer but hides the exercise. The two pauses are independent and stack.
  • Multi-answer exercises give partial credit per correct element. A wrong pick earns nothing extra but never subtracts.
  • Hint (−10%) reveals a nudge — never the answer. One hint per exercise.
  • Score at least 70% to pass. You may revise any checked exercise before the timer ends or you submit.
