HOW THIS EXAM WORKS
  • 32 exercises, realistic SOC / cloud-security work. Types include: alert-queue triage, drag and drop, log viewers with a filter box, incident workflows (branching), timeline ordering and prioritization, matching, fill-the-blanks, short answers, a fake terminal (one single-command and one multi-command drill), spot-the-mistake, and a small number of multiple-choice items (at most 5).
  • Passing score: 70%. The timer gives you 95 seconds per exercise (50:40 total). It starts when you press Start.
  • Check button: validates your answer, marks each element green (correct) or red (wrong), locks the answer for scoring, shows the explanation, and pauses the timer in review mode. The exercise stays fully visible so you can study the feedback. The timer only restarts when you press Next.
  • Manual Pause button: stops the clock but hides/blurs the exercise, so you cannot read it while paused. You can resume with the same button. The two pause types are independent and can stack.
  • Partial credit: multi-answer exercises (drag and drop, tables, fill-the-blanks, multi-select) give you points per correct element. A wrong pick earns nothing but does not subtract. Single-answer exercises are all-or-nothing.
  • Hint button: reveals a nudge for a cost of −10% of that exercise's points. It never pauses the timer.
  • Skip button: moves on and marks the exercise amber in the navigator. Skipped exercises are excluded from the score (they count in neither the points earned nor the points possible). You can come back to any exercise, including skipped ones, until time runs out or you submit.
  • Retake: if you fail you can retake; questions and answer choices are shuffled again.
