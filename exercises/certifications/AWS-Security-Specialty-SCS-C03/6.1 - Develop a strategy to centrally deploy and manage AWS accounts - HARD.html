How this exam works

  • Exercise types: scenarios, alert triage queues, drag & drop, ordering and ranking, matching, fill-in-the-blanks, log analysis with a filter box, one terminal task, branching incidents, spot-the-mistake, dual blue/red perspectives, and a small number of single-choice questions.
  • Check: press Check on every exercise to validate it. Each element is marked green (correct), red (wrong), or amber (partly right). Check locks your answer, shows the explanation, and pauses the timer in review mode — the exercise stays fully visible. Press Next to resume the clock.
  • Manual Pause: the Pause button freezes the timer but blurs the exercise, so you cannot study while it runs. The two pause types are independent and can stack.
  • Skip: skipped exercises are flagged amber. You can return to them until the timer ends. Skipped exercises are excluded from scoring — they remove their points from both what you earned and what was possible.
  • Partial credit: multi-answer exercises award points per correct element. A wrong pick earns nothing extra and subtracts nothing.
  • Hint: one hint per exercise reveals a nudge (never the answer) at a cost of −10% on that exercise.
  • Results: you need 70% of the non-skipped points to pass. A full report explains every answer. You can retake the exam with reshuffled questions and choices.
