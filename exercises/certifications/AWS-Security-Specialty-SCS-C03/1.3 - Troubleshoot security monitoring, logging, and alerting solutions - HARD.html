How this exam works

  • Format: 30 realistic exercises at MEDIUM level. Each one shows a clearly labelled SCENARIO block, a separate QUESTION block, and a labelled ANSWER area.
  • Exercise types: multiple choice, multi-select, drag & drop, ordering / risk ranking, matching, fill in the blank, log viewer with a live filter box, SOC queue triage, branching incident steps, a multi-command CLI terminal, and spot-the-mistake.
  • Check: press Check to grade. It locks the answer, shows per-part marks and the explanation, and freezes the clock in review mode. The clock only runs again when you press Next. Checking again later pauses it again.
  • Manual pause: the Pause button hides and blurs the exercise so you cannot study while paused. Both pause reasons are independent and can stack; the clock runs again only when both are cleared.
  • Skip: Skip moves on and flags the exercise amber. Skipped exercises are excluded from scoring, from both the points and the total. You can return to them until the timer ends or you submit. If you skip everything, the exam cannot be submitted.
  • Partial credit: multi-part exercises award an equal share of 1 point per correct part. Wrong picks earn nothing but never subtract. Single-answer exercises are all-or-nothing.
  • Hints: a hint reveals a nudge, never the answer, and costs 10% of that exercise's point. One hint per exercise.
  • Scoring: pass with 70% of the points of the exercises you actually answered. The final report shows your answer, the correct answer, points, and an explanation for every exercise — including skipped ones. Retake reshuffles questions and answers.
