How this exam works

  • Goal: master the keywords of objective 2.1 — design and test an incident response plan, prepare AWS for incidents, validate the plan, and automate remediation.
  • Exercise types: single-choice and multi-select questions, drag & drop, ordering, prioritization ranking, matching, fill-in-the-blank, a terminal emulator, branching incident decisions, alert triage, a log viewer with a filter box, and spot-the-mistake code reviews.
  • Check validates your answer, marks each element green (correct) / red (wrong) / amber (missed), shows the explanation, locks the answer for scoring, and pauses the timer in review mode. The content stays fully visible. Only pressing Next restarts the clock. Press Revise to edit a locked answer (a new Check re-pauses).
  • Pause (manual) is separate: it blurs and hides the exercise and freezes the clock until you resume. The two pauses stack.
  • Skip moves on without scoring (amber marker). You can return to any skipped exercise until you submit. Skipped exercises are excluded from scoring — removed from both earned points and possible points.
  • Partial credit: multi-answer exercises score per correct element (for example 3 right out of 4 = 3/4 of the points). Single-answer exercises are all-or-nothing.
  • Hint gives a nudge, never the answer. One hint costs 10% of that exercise's points.
  • Submit from the button next to the navigator at any time; at least one checked exercise is required. Passing score is 70%. On retake, questions and answer choices are shuffled again. All controls are keyboard reachable; focus is always visible.
