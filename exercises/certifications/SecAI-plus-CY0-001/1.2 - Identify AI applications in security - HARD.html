What this exam is

Every exercise mirrors real security work: alert queues, logs, tickets, playbooks and commands. You will meet an alert-triage queue, drag & drop, timeline ordering, matching pairs, a log viewer with a search box (Kibana/Splunk style), branching decisions, fill-in-the-blank reports, a terminal where you type real commands, spot-the-mistake snippets, priority ranking — and a few multiple-choice questions (5 at most).

How it works

  • Check grades your answer, marks every element green / red / amber, shows the explanation, and pauses the timer in review mode. Only Next restarts the clock.
  • Pause is the manual pause. While it is on, the exercise is blurred so it cannot be studied. The two pauses can stack.
  • Skip jumps ahead and marks the exercise amber. You can return to any skipped exercise before the exam ends. Skipped exercises are excluded from scoring — they count neither for you nor against you.
  • Multi-answer exercises give partial credit: every correct element earns its share of the points. Wrong picks never subtract — they simply earn nothing.
  • Hint reveals a nudge and costs 10% of that exercise's points.
  • Always press Check before moving on — an answer that was never checked earns nothing.
  • Timer: . When time ends, the exam submits itself with the same rules.
  • On retake, questions and answer choices are reshuffled.
