Mitigate adversarial risks

CompTIA SecAI+ (CY0-001) · Chapter 2.3 — defend AI models, data pipelines and inference layers against adversarial attacks. This file is the HARD severity exam: subtle distractors, near-miss answers, and multi-artefact reasoning where the obvious answer is often wrong.

How this exam works
  • 30 interactive exercises, all HARD: single-choice and multi-select questions, ordering and ranking, matching, fill-the-blanks, sorting buckets, a log viewer with a filter box, two terminal tasks and branching scenario walkthroughs.
  • Check grades the exercise, marks every element (green = correct, red = wrong, amber = a correct answer you missed), locks the answer for scoring and pauses the timer in review mode. Feedback and the explanation stay fully visible while the clock is frozen. Press Next to resume the timer.
  • Manual pause blurs the exercise so nothing can be studied while the clock is stopped. The two pauses stack; the timer runs only when both are cleared.
  • Skip jumps ahead without scoring. Skipped exercises stay reachable from the navigator until you submit; any exercise still skipped at submit is excluded from scoring — its points are removed from both your earned score and the maximum.
  • Partial credit: multi-answer exercises award points per correct element. A wrong pick never subtracts — it simply earns nothing.
  • Hint reveals a nudge (never the answer) for −10% on that exercise. You can navigate back to any exercise until the timer ends or you submit.
  • Pass mark: 70%. The results screen shows a full per-exercise report with explanations — including for skipped items. Retaking reshuffles questions and options.
