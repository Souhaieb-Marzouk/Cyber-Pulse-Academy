How this exam works

  • Check validates your answer, marks every element right or wrong, shows an explanation, and freezes the timer in review mode. The screen stays fully visible. Press Next to resume the clock.
  • Skip jumps ahead and marks the chip amber. Skipped exercises are excluded from scoring — they count in neither the earned points nor the maximum. You can return to any skipped exercise and finish it until you submit.
  • Two different pauses: the Pause button hides and blurs the exercise; the automatic Check-pause keeps everything visible so you can study the feedback. They stack and clear independently.
  • Partial credit: multi-answer exercises earn points per correct element. A wrong pick earns nothing but never subtracts. Single-answer exercises are all-or-nothing.
  • Hint reveals a nudge (never the answer) and costs 10% of that exercise's points.
  • Pass mark: 70% over non-skipped exercises. Exercises left unanswered at submit time are treated as skipped.
  • You may navigate back to any exercise until the timer ends. Retake reshuffles questions and answers.
