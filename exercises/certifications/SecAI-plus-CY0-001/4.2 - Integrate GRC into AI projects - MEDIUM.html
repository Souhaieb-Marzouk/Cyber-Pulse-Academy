What this exam is

A scenario-based practice exam for CompTIA SecAI+ objective 4.2 — Integrate GRC into AI projects: bringing governance, risk management and compliance practices into every stage of the AI lifecycle. Every exercise mirrors real work — governance reviews, risk registers, audits, vendor due diligence, AI incident response — so you master the chapter keywords by using them, not by memorising them.

How it works

Check validates the current exercise, marks each part green (correct), red (wrong) or amber (partial/warning), shows the explanation, and automatically pauses the timer in review mode. The content stays visible. Only Next restarts the clock.
Manual pause — the Pause button freezes the clock but hides the exercise behind a blur. Resume with the same button. The two pause reasons are independent and can stack.
Skip & return — Skip jumps ahead without scoring. Amber dashed entries in the navigator were skipped; go back and finish them any time before the timer ends. Skipped exercises are excluded from the final score (numerator and denominator).
Partial credit — multi-answer exercises score per correct element: 3 of 4 correct slots earn 3/4 of the points, and each element is marked individually (green correct, red wrong, amber missed or close). Single-answer exercises (one choice) are all-or-nothing.
Hint (−10%) — one hint per exercise reveals a nudge, never the answer, and costs 10% of that exercise's points.
Revise — after a Check you can unlock the exercise with Revise and answer again. Each new Check re-locks it and re-applies the review pause.
Timer — the clock auto-submits at zero. —
Submit & retake — Submit needs at least one answered exercise; skipped ones are excluded from the score and the total. After every attempt, a full retake reshuffles questions, options and word banks and restarts the clock.
