HOW THIS EXAM WORKS
  • Exercise types — triage queues, matching, drag-and-drop buckets, timeline ordering, risk ranking, log analysis with a filter box, spot-the-mistake lines, word banks, fill-in-the-blanks, a terminal task, branching decisions, and a small number of choice questions.
  • Check — validates your answer, marks every part (green correct, red wrong, amber missed), locks it for scoring, shows the explanation, and pauses the timer in review mode. The content stays visible. Only pressing Next restarts the clock.
  • Manual Pause — hides the exercise while paused (so nobody can study it). Resume with the same button. The two pauses can stack; the timer runs only when both are cleared.
  • Skip — flags the exercise amber and moves on. Skipped exercises are excluded from the score entirely (not earned, not possible). You can return to any exercise until you submit.
  • Partial credit — multi-part exercises give points per correct element. Wrong picks never subtract. Single-answer exercises are all-or-nothing.
  • Hint — one per exercise, costs 10% of that exercise's points, and gives a nudge, never the answer.
  • Pass mark 70%. The final screen reports your score once. Retake reshuffles questions and choices.
  • Keyboard friendly — every control is reachable by keyboard; drag tasks also work by click-to-select, then click-to-place. All indicators use icons and words, not colour alone.
