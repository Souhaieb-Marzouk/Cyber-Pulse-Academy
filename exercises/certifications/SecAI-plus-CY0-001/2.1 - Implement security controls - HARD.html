How this exam works

130 exercises, one sitting. A 45-minute countdown starts with the first question and auto-submits at zero. Only about 2 minutes per exercise at this severity.
2Check grades and locks. Check marks each element green, red or amber, shows the explanation, and locks the answer for scoring. A skipped press of Check gives no free review later.
3Check-pause (automatic). After Check the clock freezes in "review mode" so you can study the feedback. It resumes only when you move to another question (Next, Previous, or the navigator). Content stays visible.
4Manual Pause (yours). The Pause button hides the exercise behind a shield while the clock is stopped. Both pauses can stack; the clock runs again only when both are cleared.
5Skip and come back. Skip flags a question with an amber marker. Before you submit you can revisit any skipped or checked question. Skipped questions are excluded from both the earned score and the maximum.
6Partial credit. Multi-answer tasks (multi-select, slots, ordering, blanks, multi-step terminal, branching) score per correct element. Wrong picks earn nothing and subtract nothing. Single-answer tasks are all-or-nothing.
7Hints cost. One hint per exercise reveals a nudge (never the answer) and cuts that exercise's maximum by 10%.
8Pass mark 70%. Retake reshuffles everything — question order, answer order, word banks. Indicators are defanged (10[.]0[.]0[.]1, hxxp://) as in real SOC work.
