How this exam works

  • Realistic scenario work — every exercise puts you on the job: SOC triage, pentest recon, log review, hardening. Read the SCENARIO block, then the QUESTION block, then answer in the ANSWERS block.
  • Check validates your answer, marks each element (✓ right / ✗ wrong / ⚠ missed), locks it for scoring and pauses the timer in review mode — the content stays visible so you can study the feedback. The timer restarts only when you press Next.
  • Pause (manual) hides/blurs the exercise while paused. The two pauses are independent and can stack.
  • Skip jumps to the next exercise and flags this one. You can come back to skipped exercises until time ends. At the end, skipped (and never-checked) exercises are excluded from both score and total.
  • Partial credit — multi-answer exercises give you a share of the points for each correct element you identify. Single-answer exercises are all-or-nothing.
  • Hint — reveals a nudge, never the answer. Each hint used costs 10% of that exercise's points.
  • Timer — when it reaches zero the exam submits itself. Passing mark: 70% over the counted exercises.
