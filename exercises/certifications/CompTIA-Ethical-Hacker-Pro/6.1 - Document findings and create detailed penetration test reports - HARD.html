How this exam works

  • You get 30 exercises. Each one shows a SCENARIO block, then a QUESTION block, then the ANSWER area. The question is never hidden inside the data.
  • Exercise types: single choice, multi-select, matching, drag & drop sorting, timeline ordering, fill-in-the-blank, terminal commands, log analysis with a filter box, decision scenarios, and spot-the-mistake.
  • Use the numbered grid under the progress bar to jump between exercises in any order.
  • This file is the MEDIUM severity exam. Every exercise inside matches that level.

Check, Revise, Skip, Hints

  • Check grades and locks your answer, marks each part green / red / amber, and shows the explanation. Locked answers can be edited again with Revise; the new Check re-grades.
  • Skip flags an exercise (amber S in the grid) and moves on. Skipped exercises are excluded from scoring — you can come back to them until you submit or time runs out.
  • Hint reveals a nudge, never the answer. Each hint lowers that exercise's value by 10% (max 2 hints).
  • Partial credit: multi-part exercises award points per correct part. Wrong picks earn nothing extra and subtract nothing.

Timer — two different pauses

  • You have 75 minutes. At 0 the exam ends and is scored automatically.
  • Manual Pause blurs the exercise and freezes the timer until you press Resume.
  • Check-pause: after pressing Check the timer freezes for review, the exercise stays fully visible, and the clock restarts only when you press Next. The two pauses can stack.
  • Pass mark: 70%. Your result is recorded once when the results screen appears. On a retake, everything is reshuffled and reset.
