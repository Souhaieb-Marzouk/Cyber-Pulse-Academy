How this exam works

  • Check validates your answer, marks every element green (correct), red (wrong) or amber (missed / partial), locks the answer for scoring and pauses the timer in review mode. The exercise stays fully visible so you can read the explanation. The timer resumes only when you press Next (or move to another question).
  • Manual Pause freezes the clock but hides the exercise, so you cannot study while paused. You can resume with the same button. Both pauses can stack.
  • Skip jumps to the next question without any pause. Skipped questions are marked amber in the navigator and are excluded from the final score (from both earned and possible points). You can come back to them any time before the timer ends.
  • Partial credit: multi-answer exercises (multi-select, drag and drop, matching, ordering, branching) score per correct element, not all-or-nothing. Wrong picks earn nothing extra and subtract nothing.
  • Hints: one hint per question reveals a nudge (never the answer) and costs 10% of that question's points.
  • Revise: after checking, you may unlock a question and answer again. Re-checking applies the review-mode pause again.
  • Pass mark: 70%. At the end you get a full per-question report with explanations.
