How this exam works

  • Goal: master the wireless-testing keywords of this chapter through realistic SOC / pentest work — not textbook recall. Every exercise is a small piece of a real job.
  • Three blocks per exercise: the SCENARIO (setup only), the QUESTION (what you must decide), and the ANSWERS (the labelled interactive area). You never have to guess what is being asked.
  • Check validates your answer, shows which parts were right or wrong (green / red / amber, plus an icon and a word — never colour alone), and pauses the timer in review mode while the exercise, feedback and explanation stay visible. The timer resumes only when you press Next.
  • Pause (manual) freezes the clock but hides/blurs the exercise so it cannot be studied while paused. Both pauses can stack.
  • Skip jumps to the next exercise and flags this one (amber marker in the navigator). You can come back to skipped exercises any time before the timer ends. At scoring, skipped exercises are excluded from numerator and denominator — your percentage counts only what you answered.
  • Partial credit: multi-answer exercises (multi-select, drag & drop, matching, ordering, multi-command terminal) award points per correct element. A wrong extra choice never subtracts points — it just earns nothing. Single-answer exercises are all-or-nothing.
  • Hint reveals a nudge (never the answer) and costs 10% of that exercise’s points (max 30%).
  • Revise unlocks a checked exercise so you can change it, but every new Check re-applies the timer-pause rule.
  • Retake: fail and you can retake — questions and choices reshuffle, everything resets.
