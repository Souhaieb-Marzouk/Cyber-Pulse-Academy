How this exam works

  • Three clear blocks per exercise. The Scenario panel sets the situation, the Artefact panel holds the logs, tables or configs, the Question panel says exactly what to do, and the Your answer panel holds the controls.
  • Check. Every exercise has a Check button. Check grades your answer, marks each element one by one (green tick = correct, red cross = wrong, amber tilde = missed or partial), shows the explanation, locks that answer for scoring, and pauses the clock in review mode. The exercise stays fully visible so you can study it.
  • Next resumes the clock. Only the Next button (or leaving the exercise in any other way) ends review mode. Pressing Check again, editing, or reading the explanation does not resume the timer.
  • Manual pause is different. The Pause button hides the exercise behind a blur, so you cannot keep studying it. Manual pause and review mode can stack; the clock restarts only when both are cleared.
  • Revise. After Check, press Revise to unlock an answer. Your locked score stays until you press Check again.
  • Skip. Skip moves on and flags the exercise amber in the navigator. Skipped exercises are excluded from scoring: at the end the percentage is earned points divided by the points of the exercises you did not skip. You may return to any skipped exercise and finish it while the clock is still running. If everything is skipped, the exam cannot be submitted.
  • Partial credit. Multi-answer exercises (multiple answers, matching, drag and drop, ordering, ranking, terminal tasks, decision paths, fill in the blanks, spot the mistake) are scored element by element. Four correct slots out of six earns four sixths of the points. Wrong picks never subtract points.
  • Hint. One hint per exercise shows a nudge, not the answer, and costs 10% of that exercise's points. A hint never pauses the clock by itself.
  • Results. At the end you get the total, the per-exercise report with your answer, the correct answer, the points earned and a full explanation — including the exercises you skipped.
