How this exam works

  • Real work, not recall. Every exercise is a SOC, CTI or threat‑hunting work item: alert queues, tickets, intel reports, detection rules, policies, log and telemetry evidence and a fake shell.
  • Four labeled blocks per exercise. SCENARIO (the setup), EVIDENCE (the artifacts), QUESTION (what you must decide) and an answer area whose label tells you how to answer: choose, drag, order, match, fill, type, click or decide.
  • Check button. Press Check to validate your answer. Each element is marked green (correct), red (wrong) or amber (missed), the explanation opens, and the answer is locked for scoring. Press Revise if you want to change a locked answer.
  • Two different pauses. Check-pause starts automatically when you press Check: the exercise, its marks and its explanation stay fully visible and the clock freezes so you can read them — it ends only when you leave the exercise with Next. The manual Pause button is different: it hides and blurs the exercise. If both are active they stack, and the timer restarts only when both are cleared.
  • Partial credit. Multi-answer exercises (multi-select, drag and drop, matching, ordering, fill-in-the-blanks, terminal tasks) earn points per correct element, so a partly right answer still scores. Wrong selections never subtract points. Single-answer exercises stay all-or-nothing.
  • Only Checked exercises are scored. An exercise counts only after you press Check. Skipped or never-Checked exercises are removed from the score and from the maximum, so they can neither help nor hurt you. If you skip everything, the exam refuses to submit.
  • Go back freely. While the timer runs you can open any exercise from the question map, finish a skipped one, or revisit a Checked one and revise it.
  • Hints cost points. One hint per exercise reveals a nudge (never the answer) and makes that exercise worth 10% less. A hint never starts the review pause by itself.
  • Keyboard and screen reader friendly. Every control is reachable with Tab and operable with Enter or Space, focus is always visible, and each state carries a word or a symbol, not only a color.
  • All indicators are defanged (for example hxxp://evil[.]example and 10[.]20[.]30[.]40). This is a professional habit.
