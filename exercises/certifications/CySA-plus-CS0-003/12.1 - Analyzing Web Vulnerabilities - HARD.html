How this exam works

  • 30 realistic exercises in a shuffled order: multiple choice, multi-select, drag & drop categorisation, matching, ordering/ranking, fill-in-the-blank, an interactive terminal, log analysis with a live filter, branching scenarios, spot-the-mistake and dual blue/red-team perspectives. Every exercise matches this file's severity.
  • Check validates your answer, marks each element green/red/amber, shows the explanation, and pauses the timer in review mode — the content stays visible. The timer resumes only when you press Next. Press Revise to unlock a checked answer and change it; you can also press Check again later — the review pause applies again.
  • Pause (manual) hides and blurs the exercise until you press it again. The two pause reasons stack: the clock only runs when both are cleared.
  • Skip jumps ahead and marks the exercise amber. You can return to skipped exercises any time before the clock runs out. Skipped exercises are excluded from scoring — neither their points nor yours are counted. Submitting with everything skipped is refused.
  • Partial credit: multi-answer exercises score per correct element (a 4-of-5 answer earns 4/5 of the points). Single-answer exercises are all-or-nothing. Wrong selections never subtract points.
  • Hints: each hint costs 10% of that exercise's points. There are two hints per exercise.
  • Score: the counter updates live as you Check. The pass mark is 70%. At the end you get a per-exercise report with correct answers and explanations. Retake reshuffles everything.
  • Keyboard friendly: every control works with Tab/Enter, focus is always visible, and animations respect your reduced-motion preference.
