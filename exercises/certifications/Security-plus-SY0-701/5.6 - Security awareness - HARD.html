How this exam works

  • Three blocks per exercise: the SCENARIO (the setup and evidence), the QUESTION (what you must decide), and the ANSWERS (how you respond). They never mix.
  • Check validates your answer, marks every element green (correct), red (wrong) or amber (missed), locks it for scoring and shows the explanation. Checking automatically pauses the timer in review mode: the content stays fully visible so you can learn from it. The timer resumes only when you press Next.
  • Manual pause is different: the Pause button blurs the exercise so you cannot study it while the clock is frozen. The two pauses can stack; the clock only runs when both are cleared.
  • Revise unlocks a checked exercise so you can change your answer and press Check again. Your last checked answer is the one that counts.
  • Skip jumps to the next exercise and marks the navigator in amber. Skipped exercises are excluded from scoring (removed from both your points and the maximum). You can return to them any time before the timer ends.
  • Partial credit: multi-answer exercises are scored per element. Three correct slots out of four earn 3/4 of the points. Single-answer exercises are all-or-nothing.
  • Hint: one nudge per exercise, costing 10% of that exercise's points. It never reveals the answer.
  • Exercise types: choose one, choose all, drag & drop, match pairs, order steps, rank by risk, fill the blanks, live terminal, spot the mistake, decision paths, triage queues, log viewer with filter, and dual blue/red-team views.
  • The exam is fully offline. Nothing you type leaves this page.
