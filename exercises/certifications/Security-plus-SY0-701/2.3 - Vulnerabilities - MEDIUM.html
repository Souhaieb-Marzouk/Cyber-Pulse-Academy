How this exam works

  • Scenario, question, answers always sit in three separate blocks. Read the SCENARIO first, then the QUESTION, then answer in the ANSWER block.
  • Exercise types: single choice, multiple answer, drag & drop classifying and sorting, ordering, matching, fill in the blank, log analysis, and two short terminal tasks in a training shell.
  • Check validates your answer, marks every element green (correct), red (wrong) or amber (partial), locks that answer for scoring, and shows the explanation.
  • Check-pause: pressing Check freezes the clock. The exercise stays fully visible so you can read the explanation. The clock starts again only when you press Next.
  • Manual pause: the header Pause button hides the exercise (blur) and freezes the clock. Both pauses can be active at the same time; the clock runs only when both are cleared.
  • Skip moves you on without scoring that exercise. Skipped exercises are shown with an amber marker and you can come back to them until the clock ends. Skipped exercises are removed from the final score (both the points you earned and the points possible).
  • Partial credit: when an exercise has more than one correct element, points are split evenly across the elements. Three of four right earns 75% of that exercise's points.
  • Revise unlocks a checked answer so you can change it. Check again to re-lock it.
  • Hints give a nudge, never the answer, and cost 10% of that exercise's points each time.
