How this exam works

  • Check validates your answer, marks every element green (correct), red (wrong) or amber (missed / partial), locks the answer for scoring, shows the explanation and starts a review pause: the timer freezes but everything stays visible. The timer resumes only when you press Next.
  • Skip jumps ahead and marks the exercise amber in the navigator. Skipped exercises are excluded from the score — from both the points earned and the points possible. You can navigate back to them at any time before the timer ends.
  • Revise unlocks an exercise you already checked so you can change the answer. Every new Check re-locks it and re-applies the review pause.
  • Pause is the manual pause: it blurs the exercise and freezes the timer until you press Resume. It is independent from the review pause — the two can stack, and the timer only runs when both are cleared.
  • Partial credit: multi-answer exercises earn one share of points per correct element; a wrong pick earns nothing and costs nothing. Single-answer exercises are all-or-nothing.
  • Hint reveals a nudge (never the answer) and costs 10% of that exercise's points.
  • HARD severity: subtle distractors, near-miss answers and multi-artefact reasoning (logs plus tickets plus configs). Terminal exercises describe only the operational goal — the tool, the flags and the command are yours to choose.
