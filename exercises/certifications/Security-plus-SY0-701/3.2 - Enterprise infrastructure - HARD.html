Enterprise infrastructure — practice exam
Difficulty: HARD

Before you start

This is a HARD exam on Security+ chapter 3.2, Enterprise infrastructure: applying security principles to infrastructure considerations, control selection, and secure communication and access. It is scored, timed and reported like a real certification attempt.

Passing score

You need 70% or more. The pass mark is shown here and in the exam data. The result screen says PASS or FAIL.

Exercise types

Scenario decisions, single-answer and multi-answer choice, drag or tap to sort, drag to categorise, matching, fill in the blanks, a terminal, a log viewer and branching response steps.

Check button

Press Check on every exercise. It marks each element green (right), red (wrong) or amber (missed or partly right), shows the explanation, and locks your answer for scoring. Press Revise to unlock it.

Partial credit

Exercises with several correct elements are scored per element. Three right slots out of four earn three quarters of the points. Wrong picks never subtract points.

Skip and come back

Skip moves on without scoring. The navigator marks skipped exercises in amber. While the clock runs you can go back and finish them. Anything still skipped at the end is left out of the score completely.

Two kinds of pause

Pause hides and blurs the exercise so you cannot study it; press it again to resume. Check-pause is automatic after Check: the clock freezes but the exercise, the marks and the explanation stay visible. Only pressing Next restarts the clock after a Check.

Hint

One hint per exercise. It nudges you toward the right keyword group and costs 10% of that exercise's points. A hint never pauses the clock; only Check does.

Keyboard and touch

Everything works with the keyboard: Tab to any control, Enter or Space to activate. For drag-and-drop you can also tap an item and then tap its target. Arrow Up and Arrow Down reorder a row.
