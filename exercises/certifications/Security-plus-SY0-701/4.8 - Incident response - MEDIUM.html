Incident Response — MEDIUM practice exam

Scenario-based practice exam for CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701), Objective 4.8 — Incident response: implementing processes, training, testing, root cause analysis, threat hunting, and digital forensics. This file is the MEDIUM tier. Work through it like a real shift: read the artefacts, decide, and defend your call.

30 questions · 60 minutes · pass mark 70% · severity: MEDIUM
HOW TO ANSWER
  • Each exercise has three separated blocks: SCENARIO, QUESTION, ANSWERS.
  • Check grades your answer, marks each element green (correct) or red (wrong), locks it for scoring, and pauses the timer in review mode so you can read the explanation without losing time.
  • Review mode ends only when you press Next. Checking again or editing answers never restarts the clock.
  • Revise unlocks a checked exercise so you can change your answer and Check again.
  • Hint reveals a nudge, not the answer. Each hint costs −10% of that exercise's points.
TIMER, SKIP & SCORING
  • The exam timer runs for 60 minutes. It has two independent pauses: the manual Pause button (blurs the exercise) and the automatic Check-pause (review mode, content visible). They can stack.
  • Skip jumps to the next exercise and marks it amber in the navigator. You can return to skipped exercises until the timer ends.
  • Skipped exercises are excluded from scoring: they count in neither the earned points nor the total possible.
  • Multi-answer exercises give partial credit per correct element; wrong picks simply earn nothing.
  • At the end you get a full report: your answer, the correct answer, points, and an explanation for every exercise — including skipped ones.
