Resilience and recovery — practice exam
Difficulty: MEDIUM

Before you start

This is a MEDIUM exam on Security+ chapter 3.4, Resilience and recovery: high availability, recovery site choices, testing the plan, power protection, platform diversity, backups, and continuity of operations. Every exercise is a piece of real work — a ticket, a runbook, a design review, a drill report — and it is scored, timed and reported like a real certification attempt.

Passing score

You need 70% or more. The pass mark is shown here and in the exam data. The result screen says PASS or FAIL.

Exercise types

Scenario decisions, single-answer and multi-answer choice, drag or tap to sort an order, drag to categorise, matching, fill in the blanks from a word bank, a terminal, a log viewer with a filter, and branching response steps.

Check button

Press Check on every exercise. It marks each element green (right), red (wrong) or amber (missed or partly right), shows the explanation, and locks your answer for scoring. Press Revise to unlock it.

Partial credit

Exercises with several correct elements are scored per element. Three right slots out of four earn three quarters of the points. Wrong picks never subtract points.

Skip, Previous and come back

Skip moves on without scoring and does not stop the clock. The navigator marks skipped exercises in amber. While the clock runs you can return with Previous, the navigator, or Alt+Left and Alt+Right, and finish them. Anything still skipped at the end is left out of the score completely.

Two kinds of pause

Pause hides and blurs the exercise so you cannot study it; press it again to resume. Check-pause is automatic after Check: the clock freezes but the exercise, the marks and the explanation stay visible. Next restarts the clock after a Check; so does leaving that exercise with Previous or the navigator, because you have stopped reviewing it.

Hint

One hint per exercise. It opens an amber HINT panel that nudges you toward the right idea without giving the answer, and it costs 10% of that exercise's points. A hint never pauses the clock; only Check does.

Keyboard and touch

Everything works with the keyboard: Tab to any control, Enter or Space to activate. For drag-and-drop you can also tap an item and then tap its target. Arrow Up and Arrow Down reorder a row.
