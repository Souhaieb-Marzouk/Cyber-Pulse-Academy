Asset management — practice exam

This is a full certification-style exam on Objective 4.2 Asset management: acquisition, disposal, assignment, and monitoring/tracking of hardware, software, and data assets. It behaves like a real exam: a timer runs, some exercises accept more than one answer, and you get partial credit for the parts you get right.

How to take it — read once

  • Three blocks per exercise. Every exercise shows a SCENARIO block (the setup and the artefacts), a QUESTION block, and an ANSWERS block. The question is never hidden inside the logs.
  • Exercise types. SOC alert triage, log and artefact analysis with a Kibana-style filter, drag & drop, matching, timeline/lifecycle ordering, risk ranking, fill in the blank, a terminal emulator where you type the real command, "spot the mistake" config and register reviews, branching response scenarios, and a few multiple-choice questions. No two exercises are the same shape.
  • Check button. Every exercise has a Check button. It grades your answer, marks each element green (correct), red (wrong) or amber (missed / partial), locks the answer for scoring, and reveals the explanation.
  • Timer pause 1 — Check-pause. Pressing Check freezes the clock. The exercise and the feedback stay fully visible so you can study them — this is review mode, not a hidden pause. The clock restarts only when you press Next. Pressing Check again, editing your answer or reading the explanation does not restart it.
  • Timer pause 2 — manual Pause. The Pause button freezes the clock and blurs/hides the exercise, so you cannot study it while paused. The two pauses stack: the clock only runs when both are cleared.
  • Skip. Every exercise has a Skip button. Skipped exercises are marked amber in the navigator, excluded from scoring (their points are removed from the total as well as from your score), and you can come back and finish them at any time while the timer is still running. If everything is skipped, the exam refuses to submit.
  • Partial credit. Multi-answer exercises (multi-select, drag & drop slots, ordering, matching, several blanks, several terminal commands) award points per correct element. 3 of 4 slots right = 0.75 of the point. Wrong picks never subtract points.
  • Hint. One hint per exercise is available from the Hint button. It costs 10% of that exercise's points and shows a nudge, never the answer.
  • Revise. After a Check you may press Revise to change your answer; the next Check re-locks it and starts a new Check-pause.
  • Report. At the end you get the full report: your answer, the correct answer, the points earned (including partial), the label full / partial / zero / skipped, and a focus note telling you exactly what to revise. You can then retake the exam with reshuffled questions and answer choices.
The timer starts the moment you press Start.
