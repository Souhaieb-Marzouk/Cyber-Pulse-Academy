How this exam works

  • This is the HARD paper. Artefacts are ambiguous on purpose: the highest CVSS score is often not the answer, and several wrong options are true statements that simply do not fit the evidence.
  • Scenario, question, answers always sit in three separate blocks. Read the SCENARIO first, then the QUESTION, then answer inside the ANSWER block.
  • Exercise types: single choice, multiple answer, drag & drop classifying and sorting, ordering, matching, fill in the blank, log analysis, one dual blue-team / red-team exercise, and one terminal task where the tool is not named for you.
  • Check validates your answer, marks every element green (correct), red (wrong) or amber (partial), locks that answer for scoring, and opens the explanation.
  • Check-pause: pressing Check freezes the clock and keeps the exercise fully visible so you can study the explanation. The clock starts again only when you press Next.
  • Manual pause: the header Pause button blurs the exercise and freezes the clock. Both pause reasons can be active together; the clock runs only when both are cleared.
  • Skip moves on without scoring that exercise, flags it amber, and keeps it available until the clock ends. Skipped exercises are removed from the final score: both the points earned and the points possible.
  • Partial credit: when an exercise has several correct elements, its points are split evenly across them. Three of four right earns 75% of that exercise's points.
  • Revise unlocks a checked answer. Check again to re-lock it.
  • Hints give a nudge, never the answer, and cost 10% of that exercise's points each time.
  • Terminal task: the tool is not named for you. Read the operational goal, pick the tool and the flags yourself, and type one command.
