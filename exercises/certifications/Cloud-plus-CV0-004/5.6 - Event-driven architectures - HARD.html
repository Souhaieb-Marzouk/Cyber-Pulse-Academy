Event-driven architectures — practice exam

How this exam works
  • 30 scenario-based exercises on event-driven architectures in the cloud. Each one shows a clearly labelled SCENARIO block, a QUESTION block, and an ANSWERS area.
  • Exercise types: single choice, multi-select, matching, ordering, fill-in-the-blank with a word bank, classification/triage, spot-the-mistake, decision walkthrough, a mock terminal, and a log viewer with a filter box.
  • Exercises are worth 10–12 points. Multi-part exercises give partial credit: every correct element earns its share of the points.
  • Pass mark: 70%.
Rules you must know
  • Check validates and locks your answer, marks each element (green correct, red wrong, amber missed), shows the explanation, and pauses the timer in review mode — content stays visible. Only Next restarts the clock.
  • Pause (manual) hides the content while the clock is stopped. Resume brings it back. The two pauses can stack.
  • Skip moves on without scoring. A skipped exercise is excluded from both your points and the total — and you can return to it until the timer ends.
  • Hint: each hint costs 10% of that exercise's points (max 2 per exercise). It nudges; it never answers.
  • Submit at any time with Finish & submit. Answered but unchecked exercises are scored as they stand; exercises left without any answer are not counted.
  • Retake reshuffles exercises and answers and resets everything.
