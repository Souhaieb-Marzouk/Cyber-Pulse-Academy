How this exam works

  • Goal: master every keyword of chapter 2.2 through realistic work situations — pipelines, templates, state files, drift reports and incident calls.
  • Timer: you have 60 minutes total. The clock never stops for thinking — but it does pause in the two cases below.
  • Check validates your answer, shows what was right and wrong, and pauses the timer in review mode (content stays visible). The timer restarts only when you press Next (or move to another exercise).
  • Manual Pause (button, top right) also stops the clock, but hides the exercise so you cannot read while paused. The two pauses stack; both must be cleared for the timer to run.
  • Skip jumps past an exercise. Skipped items are marked amber and are excluded from scoring — they count in neither your points nor the total. You can return to them until the timer ends; checking one removes its skipped flag.
  • Partial credit: multi-answer exercises (several slots, pairs, steps, or checkboxes) score per correct element. A wrong extra pick does not subtract — you keep the points for what you got right. Single-answer items are all-or-nothing.
  • Hint: one nudge per exercise, but it costs −10% of that exercise's points.
  • Revise: after checking, you may unlock an exercise and change your answer — each new Check re-pauses the timer in review mode.
  • Pass at 70% over non-skipped exercises. Failing offers a full retake with reshuffled questions.
