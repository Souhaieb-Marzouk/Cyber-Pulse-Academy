Deployment issues — diagnosing and resolving deployment problems

This practice exam puts you on the job: broken release pipelines, failed blue-green cutovers, stuck cloud formations, throttled APIs, unreachable load balancer targets, and pods that refuse to start. Every exercise is built from realistic artefacts — deployment logs, orchestrator events, templates, tickets — so you practise the real skill: find the root cause, then choose the right fix.

Keywords covered

Every keyword below is exercised at least once in this file. Master these and the 6.1 objective is yours.

How this exam works

  • 30 exercises, 75 minutes, pass mark 70%. The timer starts when you press Start and shows a countdown in the header.
  • Exercise types: branching scenarios, log analysis with a filter box, drag & drop, matching, ordering, priority ranking, fill-the-blanks, spot-the-bug, a terminal emulator, dual blue-team/red-team perspectives, short answers and a few multiple choice questions.
  • Check validates your answer, marks each element right or wrong inline, locks the answer for scoring, and pauses the timer in review mode (content stays visible). The review pause ends only when you press Next.
  • Manual Pause is separate: it hides the exercise while paused so nobody can study mid-exam. Both pauses can stack; the clock only runs when both are cleared.
  • Skip jumps to the next exercise and flags this one as skipped. Skipped exercises are excluded from scoring (out of both your points and the maximum). You can return to any skipped or answered exercise until the timer ends or you submit.
  • Partial credit: multi-answer exercises award points per correct element, not all-or-nothing. A wrong pick earns nothing extra but never subtracts.
  • Hints cost 10% of that exercise's points each time you open one. A hint nudges; it never gives the answer away.
  • Revise unlocks a checked answer so you can change it and Check again.
  • At the end you get a full report: your answer, the correct answer, earned points, and an explanation for every exercise — including skipped ones.
