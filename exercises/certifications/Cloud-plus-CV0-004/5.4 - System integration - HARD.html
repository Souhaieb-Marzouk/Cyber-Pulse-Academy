How this exam works

  • Answer, then Check. Every exercise has a Check button. Your answer only counts for scoring after you press Check. You can go back to any exercise and press Revise, change the answer, and Check again.
  • Partial credit. Multi-answer exercises (multi-select, matching, drag & drop, ordering, cloze, terminal) earn points per correct element. A wrong pick earns nothing, but never subtracts.
  • Check-pause. After Check the timer freezes in review mode: feedback, marks and the explanation stay fully visible so you can learn. The timer resumes only when you press Next.
  • Manual pause. The Pause button freezes the timer but hides the exercise while it is paused. The two pauses can stack; both must clear before the clock runs again.
  • Skip. Skipped exercises are excluded from scoring entirely — from your points and from the total. You can return to them any time until the timer ends or you finish.
  • Hints. Each hint reveals a nudge (never the answer) and costs 10% of that exercise's points.
  • Navigation. Use Prev/Next or the number grid. On the last exercise, Next submits the exam.
  • Results. You need 70% of the non-skipped points to pass. The final report explains every exercise — including the skipped ones.
