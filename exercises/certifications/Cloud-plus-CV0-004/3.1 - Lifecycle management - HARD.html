How this exam works

  • 30 exercises, 10 points each.
  • Check validates the exercise, marks every part (green = correct, red = wrong, amber = partly right or missed) and shows the explanation. Checking also pauses the timer in review mode: the exercise stays fully visible so you can learn from the feedback. The timer resumes only when you press Next.
  • Pause (top button) is different: it stops the clock but hides the exercise behind a blurred screen. Both pauses can stack; the clock only runs when both are cleared.
  • Skip jumps to the next exercise without answering. Skipped exercises are excluded from scoring (neither earned nor possible points) and you can return to them any time before submitting.
  • Partial credit: multi-part exercises (drag & drop, ordering, matching, blanks, multi-select, terminal, simulations) award a share of the points per correct part. Single-choice questions are all-or-nothing.
  • Hint: one per exercise, reveals a nudge (never the answer) and costs 10% of that exercise's points.
  • Timer: 60 minutes for the whole file. When time runs out, the exam submits itself. You can also press Finish & Results early.
  • Exercise types: scenario simulations with branching, drag & drop, log analysis with a filter box, a terminal emulator, timeline ordering, prioritization, matching, fill-in-the-blank, spot-the-mistake, dual-perspective questions, and a small number of multiple-choice questions with tricky distractors.
  • Retake: after the results screen you can retake the exam; questions and answer choices are shuffled again.
