How this exam works

  • Check validates your answer, shows marks and the explanation, and pauses the timer in review mode. The timer restarts only when you press Next.
  • Manual Pause blurs the exercise and freezes the clock. Resume with the same button. Both pauses can stack.
  • Skip jumps to the next exercise. You can return to skipped exercises any time before the exam ends. Skipped exercises are excluded from the score (removed from numerator and denominator).
  • Partial credit: multi-answer exercises earn points per correct element. One wrong pick does not cancel a right one.
  • Hint costs 10% of that exercise's points and reveals a nudge — never the answer.
  • Revise unlocks a checked exercise so you can change it and Check again.
  • When the timer hits zero the exam closes and marks itself automatically.
