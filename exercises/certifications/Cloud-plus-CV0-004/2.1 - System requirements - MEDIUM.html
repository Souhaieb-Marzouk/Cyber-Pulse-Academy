How this exam works

  • Many exercise types: scenario simulations, drag-and-drop, log viewers, a terminal emulator, branching incidents, fill-in-the-blank, spot-the-mistake, dual-perspective and a few multiple-choice questions.
  • Check button: validates your answer, locks it, and pauses the timer so you can study the explanation. The timer only resumes when you press Next.
  • Skip button: moves on without answering. Skipped questions are excluded from scoring (both numerator and denominator). You can return to skipped questions any time before the timer ends.
  • Manual pause: the Pause button blurs the exercise so you cannot read it while paused. Use it for breaks only.
  • Partial credit: for multi-answer exercises, you earn points per correct element (no penalty for wrong selections).
  • Hint button: reveals a nudge for a −10% cost on your final score.
  • Navigator: the grid at the bottom of the exam screen lets you jump to any question. Colours show status: green=correct, red=wrong, amber=partial/skipped.
  • Retake: on the results screen, retake shuffles question order and answer choices.
