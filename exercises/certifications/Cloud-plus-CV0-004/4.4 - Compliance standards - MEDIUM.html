About this exam

How it works

  • Check validates your answer, shows inline marks and the explanation, and pauses the timer in review mode (content stays visible). The timer restarts only when you press Next (or move to another exercise).
  • Pause is the manual pause: the exercise is hidden while paused, so the clock cannot be used to think. The two pauses stack.
  • Skip jumps to the next exercise and marks it amber in the navigator. Skipped exercises are excluded from scoring (not counted as wrong). You can return to a skipped exercise before submitting — once you Check it, it is scored again.
  • Partial credit: multi-answer exercises score per correct element. A wrong pick earns nothing for that element but never subtracts.
  • Hint reveals a nudge and costs 10% of that exercise's points.
  • Revise unlocks a checked exercise so you can change your answer, then Check again.
  • Pass mark: 70%. When time runs out, the exam submits itself. Exercises never Checked are then treated as skipped.
  • Fully keyboard operable. Drag & drop also works with mouse and touch (tap to pick up, tap the target to drop).
