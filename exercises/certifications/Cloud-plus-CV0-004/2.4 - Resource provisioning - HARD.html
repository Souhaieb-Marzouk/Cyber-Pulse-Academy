Rules

  • Every exercise shows three clearly separated blocks: Scenario, Question and the answer area. Each exercise is worth 4 points.
  • Check validates your answer, marks every part (green ✓ correct, red ✗ wrong, amber ○ correct but missed), locks the answer and shows the explanation. Multi-part exercises give partial credit: each correct part earns its share; wrong parts do not subtract.
  • Check also pauses the timer in review mode: the exercise, your marks and the explanation stay fully visible, and only the Next button resumes the clock.
  • Pause (manual) hides the exercise content while the timer is frozen; press Resume to continue. The two pause reasons are independent and stack.
  • Skip jumps to the next exercise without scoring it. Skipped exercises show an amber S marker and are excluded from the final score. You can return to any skipped exercise until the timer ends or you submit.
  • Hint costs 10% of the exercise points (one hint per exercise) and reveals a nudge, never the answer.
  • Revise reopens a checked exercise. Your last Check score stands until you press Check again; leaving the exercise restores your checked answer.
  • Press Submit exam when you are done. Unanswered exercises are excluded from scoring. When the timer runs out, the exam is submitted automatically.
  • Retake resets everything: new question order, new answer order, clean score, fresh timer.
