How this exam works

  • Every exercise is a realistic work scenario worth 10 points. Multi-answer exercises (multi-select, drag & drop, matching, ordering, triage, decision trees, dual-view) give partial credit per correct element. Single-answer exercises are all-or-nothing.
  • Check submits and scores the current exercise, marks each element right or wrong, shows an explanation, and pauses the timer in review mode — the exercise stays fully visible. The timer restarts only when you press Next.
  • Revise unlocks a checked exercise so you can change answers. Checking again re-scores it and pauses the timer again.
  • Skip moves you on immediately. Skipped exercises are marked amber in the navigator and you can return to them any time before the timer ends. At the end, skipped and never-submitted exercises are excluded from scoring (numerator and denominator).
  • Pause (manual) hides the exercise and freezes the clock; press Resume to continue. The two pause types are independent and stack — the timer runs only when both are cleared.
  • Hint (−10%) reveals a nudge, never the answer. One hint per exercise; it costs 10% of that exercise's points.
  • Submit (or the timer ending) shows your result over submitted exercises only — the exam refuses to submit while nothing is answered. You need 70% to pass. Retake reshuffles every question and answer choice.

Exercise types you will meet

SOC triage queues, a Kibana-style log viewer with filter, drag & drop sorting, timeline ordering, matching, YAML/policy completion, spot-the-line review, branching incident decisions, blue-team/red-team dual view, an interactive terminal, and a few multiple-choice questions.
