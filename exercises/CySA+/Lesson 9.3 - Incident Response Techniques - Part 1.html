Incident Response & Handling · IR-201

You are the on-call incident handler for a mid-size company. Across 25 tasks you will run a SOC shift, triage live alerts, contain a real intrusion, protect evidence for a legal case, and decide what the law requires you to do. Every task is interactive. Nothing is a passive reading exercise.

Pass mark · 80%

Each task is worth 100 points. Multi-part tasks give partial credit. Reach 80% overall to pass and get the certificate panel.

Timer

The clock is set from the number and difficulty of the tasks. You can pause, but while paused the current task is hidden so you cannot read it.

Hints cost 10%

Stuck on a task? A hint removes 10% of that task's points. It gives you a nudge, never the answer.

Exercise types you will face

  • Scenario simulations — a live SOC alert queue you must triage.
  • Branching incident — each choice changes the story of a ransomware case.
  • Drag & drop — sort actions into response phases and categories.
  • Timeline ordering — put IR phases and attack events in the right order.
  • Evidence & volatility — decide what to capture first and how to keep it legal.
  • Log and PCAP/flow analysis — read Sysmon and EDR logs, hunt in a filterable log viewer, and spot beaconing in network-flow data.
  • Terminal emulator — type the real command a responder would run.
  • Matching & fill-in-the-blank — frameworks, breach-notice rules, report writing.
  • Dual perspective — the same case from the blue team and the red team.
  • Spot the mistake & risk ranking — find the broken step, rank the queue.
  • QCM — used only a few times, with close distractors.
