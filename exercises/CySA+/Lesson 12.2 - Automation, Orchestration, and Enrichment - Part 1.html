How this exam works

  • After every answer you get a short explanation in plain English. Wrong answers also show what to study.
  • A hint costs 10% of that exercise. A hint is a nudge, never the answer.
  • The timer can be paused, but the exercise is hidden while paused, so you cannot read it in advance.
  • You may submit an unfinished exercise, but the missing parts score zero.
  • On a retake, both the exercises and the answer choices are shuffled again.
  • The score is live: 25 exercises, 100 points, pass at 80 points or more.
  • The time limit comes from the total difficulty: 36 seconds per point, so 100 points gives 60 minutes.
