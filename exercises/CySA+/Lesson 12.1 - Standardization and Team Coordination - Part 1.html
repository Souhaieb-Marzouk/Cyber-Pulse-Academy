CyberOps Coordination & Standards Specialist

This exam drops you inside a real security operations centre. You will triage an alert queue, run a ransomware response, tune detections, write handoffs, and prove your decisions to an auditor. The questions are deliberately hard: several answers look correct but fail on one detail. Work each scenario as if it were your shift.

Pass mark

You need 80% of the total points or more. The live score sits in the header at all times.

Timer

The clock covers the whole exam and is sized from the number and difficulty of the exercises. You may pause it, but while it is paused the question is hidden, so you cannot read ahead.

Hints

Stuck? You get one hint per exercise and it costs 10% of that exercise's points. A hint is a nudge, never the answer.

Exercise types you will meet

  • Alert triage and prioritisation - choose the right order under an SLA.
  • Drag and drop classification - move techniques, controls and messages into the right bucket.
  • Timeline ordering - rebuild incident, escalation and forensic sequences.
  • Log and SIEM analysis - read a realistic log with a filter box and click the proof.
  • Branching scenarios - every decision unlocks the next step and changes your score.
  • Dual perspective - see the same event once as blue team, once as red team.
  • Terminal work - type the real SIEM search in a fake shell.
  • Fill in the blank - complete an incident report or a change record from a shuffled word bank.
  • Spot the mistake - find the line in a handoff, a report or a policy that creates risk.
  • Matching - connect tools and frameworks to what they actually do.
  • Multiple choice - only 5 of the 26 exercises, with near-miss distractors.
