Rules of the exam

  • Pass mark: 80% of the total points (hint costs included). Below that, the exam is failed and you must retake it.
  • The timer is computed from the number and the difficulty of the tasks. When it reaches zero the exam closes and scores what you have.
  • You can pause the timer at any time for a break. While paused, the current task is hidden (not readable) and the clock stops.
  • Every task has 1 or 2 hints. Each hint costs 10% of that task points and only gives a nudge, never the answer.
  • Answers are submitted one task at a time. Partial credit is given for multi-part tasks (matching, ordering, triage, fills).
  • After each task you get an explanation. At the end you get a full report with a focus tip for every mistake.
  • Retaking the exam reshuffles the tasks and every answer choice.
  • Keyboard: number keys select an option, Enter checks and moves on, Esc pauses (the task is hidden while paused).
