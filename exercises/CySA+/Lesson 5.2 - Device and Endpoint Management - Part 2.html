Exam Rules

  • 24 exercises, 12 interaction formats: QCM, multi-select, matching, timeline ordering, risk ranking, policy fill-in, spot-the-mistake, log viewer with live filter, terminal emulator, branching incident scenarios, alert triage, and dual blue/red-team perspectives.
  • Passing score: 80%. Your score updates live as you check each exercise.
  • Timer: the total time is computed from the number and difficulty of the exercises. When it hits zero, the exam auto-finishes.
  • Pause is allowed — but pausing hides the whole exercise so you cannot read it while the clock is stopped.
  • Hints cost −10% of that exercise's points. A hint nudges you; it never reveals the answer.
  • Partial credit exists on most exercises — every correct piece counts.
  • After each answer you get an explanation. Checked answers are locked; you can revisit them to review.
  • On retake, questions and all answer choices are reshuffled.
⚠ Professional habit: every IP, host, and address in this exam is defanged — written as 10[.]0[.]4[.]23 or hxxp://… — so nothing can be clicked or resolved by accident.
