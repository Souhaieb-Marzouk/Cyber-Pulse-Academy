Quick glossary — terms you will meet
SIEM — a tool that gathers alerts and logs from many systems in one place.
EDR — endpoint detection and response: a security agent that watches one computer.
DDoS — a flood of fake traffic that blocks a website or service.
VPN — a protected tunnel from outside into a network.
SMB — the rules computers use to share files with each other.
ACL — access list: rules that say which traffic may pass a firewall.
SSH — a way to control a server from far away; it uses port 22.
Brute force — trying many passwords until one works.
Exfiltration — quietly copying data out of a network.
GDPR — a European privacy law; breaches must be reported within 72 hours.
RACI — a table that names who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed.
Containment — stopping an attack from spreading.
Eradication — removing the attacker and the malware.
Assume breach — planning as if attackers will get in someday.
