GRC-100 — Security Governance & Policy

Nordwind Logistics has just hired you into the Security Programme Office. Over the next 31 exercises you will run a real governance and policy workload: triage a SOC alert queue, review a policy register from a GRC console, rank a risk register, walk a ransomware incident from detection to after-action review, and defend the programme to an auditor and to the board.

How the exam works

  • One screen per exercise. Submit an answer to see immediately whether it was right, with a plain-English explanation of why — then move on. You cannot return to a submitted exercise.
  • Several exercises are composite: one case, several questions. Partial answers earn partial points.
  • Drag & drop works with the mouse; on a tablet tap the item, then tap the target instead.
  • Pause hides the exercise completely, so you cannot read the case while the clock is stopped.
  • At the end you get a full report on all 31 exercises and, at 80% or more, a certificate you can screenshot.
