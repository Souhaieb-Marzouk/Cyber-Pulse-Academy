Attack Surface Management — Field Certification

A hard, scenario-based exam for SOC and vulnerability-management analysts. Every exercise mirrors real attack surface work: triage queues, scan reports, cloud misconfigurations, shadow IT, branching incidents and policy drafting.

Exam rules

  • Every exercise gives instant feedback plus a plain-English explanation after you submit. Read it — each explanation teaches the keywords behind the task.
  • You may pause the timer, but while paused the exercise is hidden from view, so pausing cannot help you answer.
  • Drag & drop works with the mouse. On touch screens: tap a card, then tap a slot. Tap a placed card to send it back.
  • Unanswered items score zero. The exam ends automatically when the timer reaches 00:00.
  • On a retake, the exercise order and every answer choice are shuffled again — memorizing positions will not help.
  • Distractors are deliberately near-miss. If an answer looks obvious, read it twice.
