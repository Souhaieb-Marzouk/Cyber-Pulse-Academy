Rules of the exam

  • Every exercise is a work task: a SOC shift, a handover, a real config, a real log, a real ticket.
  • All exercises are hard on purpose. Several have near-miss answers that are possible but wrong in context.
  • Partial credit exists on ordering, matching, multi-select, log picking and multi-blank questions.
  • One hint per question, costing 10% of that question's points. A hint is a nudge, never the answer.
  • You can pause the timer. While paused the exercise is hidden, so pausing is not free thinking time.
  • All indicators are defanged on purpose (hxxp://, 10[.]0[.]0[.]1, evil[.]example). This is a professional habit, not a typo.
