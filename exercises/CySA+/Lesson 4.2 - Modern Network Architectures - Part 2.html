How this exam works

  • Passing score: 80% — your score updates live after every exercise.
  • One global timer sized to the number and difficulty of the exercises. You may pause it — but while paused the exercise is fully hidden, so you cannot think with the clock stopped.

  • Every answer is explained — right or wrong — in plain, simple English, with a focus tip when you miss.
  • Retake any time: questions and answer choices are reshuffled on every attempt.
