Certification Exam: Scripting for Security Analysts

You are the newest automation-focused analyst on the SOC engineering team. This exam puts you inside the work: triaging alerts, reading logs, fixing unsafe scripts, and building repeatable workflows.

Keywords covered by this exam

Every keyword below is exercised at least once. The final report shows which ones you have mastered.

Themes and progress

Exam rules

Exercises: realistic tasks across 5 themes, worth points.

Passing score: 80%. Below that you must retake the exam.

Timer: , calculated from the number and difficulty (points) of the tasks. Use Pause for a break: the question is hidden while paused.

Hints: one hint per task costs 10% of that task score. A hint is a nudge, not the answer.

Retake: questions and answer choices are shuffled again.

Task types you will meet

Drag and drop sorting, timeline ordering, log triage in a filterable viewer, a fake terminal where you type real commands, fill-in-the-blank scripts, "spot the mistake" code review, matching, risk ranking, branching incident decisions, a red/blue double perspective, and five multiple-choice items.

Indicators of compromise are defanged (for example hxxp://evil[.]example or 10[.]0[.]0[.]1) so nothing here looks like live malware infrastructure.
