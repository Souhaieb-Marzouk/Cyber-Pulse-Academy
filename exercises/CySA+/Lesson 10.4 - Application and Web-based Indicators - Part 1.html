How the exam works
  • You answer one exercise at a time. Press Check answer to lock it and see the explanation. Locked answers cannot be changed.
  • Every exercise pays partial credit where that makes sense (multi-select, sorting, ordering, fill-in-the-blank). Wrong picks cost you points.
  • A hint costs −10% of that exercise's points. One hint per exercise. Hints nudge you, they never give the answer.
  • The timer is strict. You may pause it, but while paused the questions are hidden so you cannot read them during your break.
  • When time reaches zero the exam submits itself. Questions you never checked score 0.
  • Retake reshuffles the questions and all the answer choices.
  • All indicators are defanged (10[.]0[.]0[.]1, hxxp://evil[.]example). This is the safe, professional way to store and share IOCs.
  • Time budget: 28 exercises × about 105 seconds each ≈ 50 minutes. The clock is sized to the number and the difficulty of the exercises.
