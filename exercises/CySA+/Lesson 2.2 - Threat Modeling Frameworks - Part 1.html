SEC-TM-1 · STRIDE Threat Modeling Practitioner

This exam puts you inside real work: a threat modeling session for a payment gateway, a SOC alert queue at 02:47 UTC, a GRC review where the client refuses MFA, a bucket policy that must match the model. You will not be asked to repeat definitions. You will be asked to decide, and then told exactly why.

Format

28 exercises · 87 points · 13 different exercise types. Every exercise mirrors a real role: SOC analyst, incident responder, threat model facilitator, GRC analyst, cloud engineer, detection engineer.

Time limit

65 min 50 s, computed from the number of exercises and their difficulty (about 2 min 20 s per exercise). The timer counts down. You may pause it, but while paused the exercise is hidden so you cannot think during the break.

  • Explanations are written in simple English on purpose: short sentences, plain words.
