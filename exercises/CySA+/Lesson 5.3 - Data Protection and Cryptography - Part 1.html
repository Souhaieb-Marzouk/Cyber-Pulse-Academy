DP-201 · Data Protection Practitioner

Version 2.0. A hands-on certification exam. You will work a SOC shift, triage alerts, read audit logs, type real commands, rank real risks and defend your choices to a regulator. Not a quiz about definitions.

Passing score

80% of 28 points. Below that, you must retake the exam.

Time limit

90 minutes, calculated from 28 exercises that range from quick decisions to multi-step incidents.

Pause allowed

The timer stops and the question is hidden, so you cannot read it while paused.

Hints

One hint per question. It costs -10% of that question's score and only gives a nudge, never the answer.

How scoring works

  • One-part questions are worth 1.00 point, all or nothing.
  • Multi-part items such as triage, matching, ordering and drag and drop give partial credit per correct part.
  • In a multiple-select question, a correct pick adds points and a wrong pick removes points, so guessing costs you.
  • Every answer is graded at once. You cannot change a question after you submit it.
