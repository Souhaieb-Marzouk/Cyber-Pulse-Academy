Security Analytics & Pattern Recognition

You are a newly hired analyst on the SOC floor of Northwind Logistics. This exam drops you into real shifts: triaging alert queues, reading log lines, tuning noisy rules, hunting beacons, reading a hunting dashboard and writing SIEM searches. Every exercise is built from the concepts below. There are no textbook quizzes here — only work that looks and feels like the job.

Why 75 minutes? There are 28 hard items, so the clock allows about 2.5 minutes each, plus 5 minutes of thinking time at the end. The clock is a difficulty setting, not a race: it pushes you to read patterns quickly, the way a real shift does.


Rules of the exam

  • Each item is scored immediately, with a full explanation either way.
  • Pass mark: 80%. Below that you must retake the exam.
  • One hint costs 10% of that item's points. A hint nudges you, it never gives the answer.
  • The timer is 75 minutes for 28 hard items. If time runs out, the exam is scored as it stands.
  • The Pause button freezes the clock and hides the current item, so you cannot read while paused.
  • Retaking reshuffles the items and every answer choice.

Exercise types in this exam

  • Ordering — drag stages into the right sequence.
  • Risk ranking — prioritise the alert queue.
  • Matching — tools to jobs, anomalies to causes.
  • Drag & drop classification — signatures, anomalies, IoCs.
  • Log viewer — filter and read real Windows event lines.
  • Terminal — type a real SIEM search.
  • Branching scenario — your move unlocks the next step.
  • Spot the mistake — find the flaw in a rule or a baseline.
  • Dashboard / trend reading — pick the panel that deserves a hunt.
  • Threshold tuning — move the slider on a 24-hour chart.
  • Dual perspective — the same event from blue and red team.
  • Fill in the blanks — complete an incident report.
  • Multiple choice & multi-select — with near-miss distractors.
