ⓘ What you will learn here

This unit teaches T1586.001 — Compromise Accounts: Social Media Accounts. It is a sub-technique of T1586, and the work sits in the Resource Development tactic (TA0042) on the PRE platform. The idea is takeover, not creation: instead of building a persona from scratch (that is T1585), the adversary breaks into a social media account that already exists — on a LinkedIn-style, X-style, or Telegram-style service — and keeps using it. The stolen profile brings built-in trust: real history, real connections, a real face.

Adversaries pick the account with prior reconnaissance, then get in by harvesting credentials through Phishing for Information (T1598), buying logins from third-party sites, or brute forcing reused passwords from breach dumps. After the takeover they maintain the persona — profile edits, photos, new connections — and later they spend it, above all on Spearphishing via Service (T1566.003), where the trusted account sends the malicious link.

You will meet this object in real work. A SOC analyst triages the odd DM that arrived from a colleague's account. A CTI analyst tracks the hijacked handle and the campaign behind it. A brand-risk owner runs brand monitoring and files platform reports and takedown requests. An IR responder helps the staff who replied. A red teamer shows, under authorisation, how far a trusted persona can go.

The hard truth is the page's only mitigation, M1056 Pre-compromise: no enterprise control can stop an outsider taking over an account on a third-party platform. The compensations are monitoring for the AN2002 signals — personas claiming to work for you, and recently edited accounts making many connection requests — plus staff verification habits and fast takedowns. This EASY exam drills the boundary (takeover vs build, .001 vs .002 vs .003), the trust logic, the takeover flow, and the defender basics.

⚖ Keywords covered in this exam

Object basics & boundaries

  • Compromise = takeover of an existing social media account
  • Parent T1586 · siblings .002 Email · .003 Cloud
  • T1585 builds personas; T1586 takes them over
  • Tactic: Resource Development (TA0042) · Platform: PRE

Adversary behaviour

  • Inherited trust and a ready-made network
  • Harvest logins via Phishing for Information (T1598)
  • Buy credentials from third-party sites
  • Brute force with reused breach-dump passwords
  • Maintain the persona: edits, photos, connections

Attack chain

  • Spearphishing via Service (T1566.003) spends the persona
  • Long-con chats build trust before the ask

Mitigations & defenses

  • M1056 Pre-compromise: no direct enterprise control
  • Brand monitoring, platform reporting, takedown requests

Telemetry & detection

  • AN2002: monitor social media activity about your org
  • Signal: personas claiming to work for your organization
  • Signal: recently modified accounts making numerous requests
