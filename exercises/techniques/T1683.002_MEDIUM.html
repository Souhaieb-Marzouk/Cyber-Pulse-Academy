ⓘ What you will learn here

You will learn how T1683.002 — Generate Content: Audio-Visual Content — works as a sub-technique of T1683 in the Resource Development tactic (TA0042), platform PRE. It covers the audio, image and video material adversaries build before an attack: synthetic voice recordings, deepfake videos of fabricated or impersonated individuals, fabricated profile photos and identity documents, and cloned portal visuals such as fake banking login pages and fake Microsoft Outlook login window prompts.

You will practise recognising the pieces in real work. As a SOC analyst you will meet the lure when it lands at the gateway or the proxy. As a CTI analyst you will attribute kits — NIMBUS MANTICORE's job-themed sites, decoy files and Outlook-style prompts, or APT-C-36's bank-portal clones — and map them to the downstream techniques they feed. As an IR lead you will handle a reported 'CEO' voice note. As a detection engineer you will anchor controls where media lands, because generation itself stays outside enterprise visibility. As a red teamer you will stage such media safely, inside an authorization.

You will also learn the limits. AI-assisted tools produce synthetic media at scale, at a quality that is harder to identify as inauthentic, so pixel-hunting loses to process: call-backs, out-of-band verification and a reporting culture. This MEDIUM set has 15 exercises, about 24 minutes. Each exercise connects two ideas — read the scenario, decide what to look at first, and expect every wrong option to be wrong for a specific reason.
