🎓 What you will learn here

This is the HARD exam for T1591.001 — Determine Physical Locations. The sub-technique collects where the victim is: offices, plants, data centers — and, through those places, the legal jurisdiction the organisation answers to. At HARD level the collection stops being a page fetch and becomes a campaign: site series fetched at night, adverts read twice, campus maps pulled as "assets", facility reports that match the browsing pattern.

You will work the cases with senior discipline. You will attribute artefacts to the right sub-technique when families blur (a "site lead" sentence carries a place and a person); you will read a whois record for its registered address and know exactly what it does and does not prove; you will choose the least-aggressive protective action when the drone over the site might be a hobbyist. The documented procedure — Magic Hound harvesting location from its own phishing-site visitors — anchors the recurring lesson: this collection can run entirely outside your visibility.

The defender’s half stays equally honest: M1056 minimisation works on granularity and timing because the legal floor is real; exposure regrows through adverts and press items; and the facility guard is your pre-compromise sensor. Expect the HARD traps in their natural habitat: the grand-opening press item that is an access schedule, the alarming scraping burst that is the SEO agency, and the one intervention that actually changes the observed path.
