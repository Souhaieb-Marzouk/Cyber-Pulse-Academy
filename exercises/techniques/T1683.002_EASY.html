ⓘ What you will learn here

You will learn what T1683.002 — Generate Content: Audio-Visual Content — covers. It is a sub-technique of T1683 (Generate Content) in the Resource Development tactic (TA0042), platform PRE. It is about adversaries creating or manipulating audio, image, and video content: synthetic voice recordings, altered audio or video during live calls, fabricated profile photos, and videos of fabricated or impersonated individuals. Pure written content belongs to the sibling sub-technique T1683.001.

You will learn the three production paths named on the page: manual editing tools, AI-assisted tools, and third-party synthetic services. You will also see why AI-assisted tools matter: they produce synthetic media at scale, and the output is harder to identify as inauthentic.

You will see where this matters in real work. A SOC analyst meets the finished lure in mail or chat and checks where it leads. A CTI analyst reads procedure examples like APT38's fake-photo personas and maps them to ATT&CK. An IR lead handles the fraud or phishing attempt that the media enabled. A detection engineer builds checks where the media lands, because M1056 Pre-compromise teaches that the creation itself cannot be prevented. A red teamer uses the same craft, under authorisation, to test whether staff verify out-of-band.

Daily deliverables you will practise: sorting artefacts into content types, mapping media to the downstream technique it feeds such as Phishing (T1566) or Establish Accounts (T1585), and writing the defense note: do not lean on atomic indicators of the media itself — watch where the media lands and respond there.
