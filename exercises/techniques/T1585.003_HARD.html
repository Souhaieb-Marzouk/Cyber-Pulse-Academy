📘What you will learn here

This is the HARD exam for T1585.003 — Cloud Accounts, the cloud sub-technique of T1585 Establish Accounts (Resource Development tactic, platform PRE). The scenarios now correlate artefacts the way a senior analyst would: audit chains that show an account was created to host payloads, Teams logs that expose a spoofed helpdesk, tenant signals ranked by triage value, and the detection strategy (DET0846 / AN1978) that points defenders at the exfiltration stage because the signup itself never touches victim telemetry.

You will work the real cases on the page from both sides: OilRig's Outer Space campaign ran C2 through created M365 mailboxes, MirrorFace's Operation AkaiRyū hosted payloads on OneDrive, and Storm-1811 built M365 accounts to pose as IT support in Teams. Expect near-miss answers: the obvious read is often the wrong one, "alarming-looking" supplier invites can be legitimate, and the correct response is sometimes the least aggressive one. Attacker reasoning (why cloud accounts are cheap, disposable and blending in) and defender reasoning (what is actually observable, and which controls bite) are both tested — in the same file, on the same evidence.
