📘What you will learn here

You will learn T1585.001 — Social Media Accounts, a sub-technique of the MITRE ATT&CK technique T1585 Establish Accounts. ATT&CK is a shared catalogue of adversary behaviour. A sub-technique is one specific way of doing a broader technique — here, creating and looking after fake social media accounts that the attacker will use later.

Imagine you are a new analyst. Before an attack, an adversary builds a fake profile: a name, a photo, a job history, and connections. The profile may pretend to be a friendly recruiter or an employee. Later, this persona is used to reach your staff — for example to send them a malicious link through LinkedIn or another platform.

Where you will meet this in real work: SOC analysts see the first contact as odd connection requests; threat intelligence (CTI) analysts track personas and write watchlists; threat hunters use T1585.001 to build hunts; detection engineers map the later phishing stage; brand-protection and GRC teams report fake profiles for takedown; HR and staff awareness teams warn about fake job offers. You will also learn why ATT&CK marks this technique PRE (it happens before any compromise) and why prevention is weak here.
