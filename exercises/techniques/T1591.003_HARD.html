📘 What you will learn here

This is the challenging tier for T1591.003 — Identify Business Tempo (parent T1591 — Gather Victim Org Information, tactic Reconnaissance TA0043, platform PRE). At this level the scenarios look like real tickets: several artefacts, partial information, and answers where two options are defensible in some other context but only one is correct here.

You will practice the skills a senior analyst uses: mapping adversary behaviour to the exact sub-technique (and not to its tempting neighbours), separating benign noise from recon footprints, choosing the least aggressive effective response, and writing findings that connect tempo collection (T1591.003) to its downstream effects such as timed phishing waves (T1598) or supply-chain timing (T1195, T1199).

The mitigation picture stays uncomfortable, and you must reason with it: M1056 — Pre-compromise gives no preventive control — only minimisation of exposed operational data. Detection therefore focuses on second-order effects: waves that match the organisation's rhythm, probing of public pages, and infrastructure that behaves like a collector.

Expect near-miss answers. In roughly a third of the exercises the “obvious” choice is wrong for a specific, explainable reason. Read every artefact before you commit.
