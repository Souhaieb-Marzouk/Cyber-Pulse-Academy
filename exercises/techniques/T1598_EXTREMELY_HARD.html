📘 What you will learn here

This is the expert tier for T1598 — Phishing for Information (tactic Reconnaissance TA0043, platform PRE). The artefacts are incident-commander material: M365 tenants mid-abuse, harvesting infrastructure on legitimate CDNs, vishing campaigns aimed at your helpdesk, and board members demanding verdicts in minutes.

You will face the tier's four signature problems: conflicting signals (authentication says the channel is real, content says the sender is not); a correct action with a real cost (mailbox audits with privacy approvals, tenant-wide resets at peak hours); a popular best practice that is wrong here; and at least one moment where the right answer is to gather more evidence or escalate rather than act. The page's six groups — APT28, Kimsuky, Moonstone Sleet, Scattered Spider, ShinyHunters, ZIRCONIUM — are your calibration anchors for what this technique looks like at full pressure.

The mitigation frame never moves: M1054 (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) and M1017 (training) are the ATT&CK-listed controls; everything else is detection and response craft. At this tier you must also handle the page's evasion note — Email Hiding Rules (T1564.008) on abused mailboxes — as evidence, not noise.

One terminal task carries a reduced task timer, announced on screen before it starts, exactly as senior responders face time-boxed decisions. Hints restate constraints only.
