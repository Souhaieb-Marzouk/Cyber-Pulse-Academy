ⓘ What you will learn here

This unit teaches T1078.002 — Valid Accounts: Domain Accounts — a sub-technique of T1078. It is about the abuse of credentials for domain accounts: the accounts of users, administrators and services that Active Directory Domain Services manages, with permissions configured across systems and services in the whole domain.

You will meet it in real work. As a SOC analyst you triage logons that look almost normal — an off-hours RDP session, a first-time host, a burst across many machines. As an IR lead you connect a credential-dumping alert to the domain accounts that were then reused. As a detection engineer you tune the DET0210 strategy and its analytics, pairing events like 4624, 4648, 4672 and 4728 into evidence. As a CTI analyst you map how groups and tools abused valid domain accounts. As a red teamer you exercise valid-account paths only inside a signed authorization.

Daily tools: SIEM queries and event-log tools such as wevtutil, EDR consoles, group-membership reviews, and control checklists for MFA, password policies, privileged account management, user account management and user training.

The risk to respect: valid credentials bypass access controls on VPN, OWA, remote desktop and network devices, they survive reboots without any malware, and they blend with normal user activity — which makes them hard to detect and easy to under-react to.

⚖ Keywords covered in this exam

Adversary behaviour & value

  • credential dumping (T1003) and password reuse feed valid-account abuse
  • access that survives reboots and EDR
  • Domain Admin membership means domain-wide reach
  • activity mimics the real user
  • valid creds unlock VPN, OWA, remote desktop, network devices

Telemetry & detection

  • Kerberos context: TGT/TGS tickets (4768/4769)
  • 4648 explicit-credential and 4672 special-privilege events
  • 4728 security-enabled group change
  • DET0210 — behaviour over indicators
  • AN0590 — multi-host and off-hours logons

Mitigations & controls

  • M1018 User Account Management
  • M1017 User Training for MFA pushes
