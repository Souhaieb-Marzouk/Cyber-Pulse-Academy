What you will learn here

  • This exam is about the MITRE ATT&CK sub-technique T1222.001 - Windows File and Directory Permissions Modification (technique T1222, Defense Evasion, Windows).
  • You will evaluate complete SDDL DACLs and reason about ACE flags: OI, CI, ID, NP, and the protected-DACL flag P.
  • You will analyse privilege abuse: SeTakeOwnershipPrivilege, the owner’s implicit WRITE_DAC, and ownership chaining on locked files.
  • You will read 4670 diffs like evidence: counter-forensic ACL restoration, SDDL fingerprints, and what 4670 can never tell you.
  • You will design PowerShell-only hunting with Get-Acl baseline diffing, and a layered detection stack that covers tool-ful and tool-less variants.
  • You will judge safe versus destructive recovery on trees with custom ACLs, and emulate the documented technique in a sandbox lab.

How this exam works

  • The passing score is 70%. Your percent is the credit you earned divided by the exercises you answered, rounded the normal way.
  • You will meet exercise types: single choice, multi-select, drag-and-drop sorting, multi-step questions, and terminal exercises (at most two) where you type real Windows commands in a safe fake shell.
  • Multi-select and sorting give partial credit: credit = (hits − wrong) ÷ correct answers, and it never goes below zero. A partly right answer earns part of the point.
  • The timer shows your budget: 35:00 for this exam. It runs only while you answer.
  • The Pause button stops the clock by hand (⏸ Paused). When you press Check, the exam pauses itself (⏳ Auto-paused) so reading the feedback does not burn time; pressing Next starts the clock again.
  • Every exercise has one hint. Using it costs a small penalty: −0.15 on that exercise’s credit.
  • You can skip an exercise; the clock keeps running. At the end you may review skipped exercises. Skipped exercises you never answer do not count in your score or your total.
  • When you finish, your result is shown and reported to the platform once. Then you can retake the exam.
