What you will learn here

Welcome to the EXTREMELY HARD tier of T1608.006 — SEO Poisoning, a Sub-Technique of T1608 Stage Capabilities in tactic TA0042 Resource Development, platform PRE. This is preparation work that happens before any victim is contacted: the adversary rigs the machinery that decides what a search engine shows — purchased ads plus the ranking, score and reputation its crawlers and algorithms compute — so that people find the attacker's page themselves, through their own searches. No lure message is ever sent. The victim's own query delivers the bait, which is what makes this technique so useful for the drive-by and fake-update lures in this file.

Here is the rigging in plain words. Keyword stuffing crams chosen terms into compromised sites — including hidden text a person never sees but a crawler still scores. Link rings buy or plant incoming links, — often dozens of fresh registrations pointing at one target — to fake the reputation signals crawlers count. Cloaking shows the crawler one page and people another, keyed on the browser user agent, language settings, other HTTP headers or mouse movements, usually behind evasive redirects that only the right audience is waved through. The canvas is usually compromised sites (T1584), the staged payload a Drive-by Target (T1608.004), and the click ends in Drive-by Compromise (T1189), a client exploit (T1203) or a backdoored package (T1195).

Who works this file: the SOC analyst who must resolve an alert where the crawler cache and a clean fetch disagree; the CTI analyst who audits link profiles and reports poisoned results to the search vendor; the IR lead who owns the response runbook and its takedown call; the detection engineer who keeps the DET0881 / AN2013 card honest about its limits; the GRC reviewer who builds the M1056 program on intel platforms, surface monitoring, domain watching and awareness; and the awareness owner who teaches staff that searching for software can itself be the trap.

You will meet the documented cases from the ATT&CK procedure examples: G1020 Mustard Tempest, which poisoned search results so they returned fake software updates that distributed malware, and G1059 NIMBUS MANTICORE's link-ring playbook — plus the DFIR Report Gootloader citation: purchased and planted incoming links lifting poisoned rankings, with cloaking documented alongside in the Sophos write-up. Around them sit this tier's decisions: evidence that contradicts itself, a shared host a reflex block would break, a delivery hop nobody logged, and commands safe or destructive by one character.

Jargon, explained as it appears: an evidence pack is the set of captures a case stands on; a per-audience capture fetches the same URL as a crawler and as a person to expose cloaking; re-poisoning is the same rig resurfacing on fresh domains after a takedown; a scoped takedown removes only the poisoned paths, never the whole host. Every exercise gives you a hint — it costs 0.10 credit and only restates a constraint — and a full explanation. Read it even when you were right: the why is the job.
