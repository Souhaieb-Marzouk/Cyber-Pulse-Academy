📘 What you will learn here

This exam goes one level deeper into T1591.003 — Identify Business Tempo, a sub-technique of T1591 — Gather Victim Org Information in the Reconnaissance tactic (TA0043). You already know that business tempo means the rhythm of an organisation: operational hours and days, plus the times and dates of purchases and shipments. Here you connect that rhythm to what it enables and to what defenders can do about it.

Think of your daily work as a SOC or CTI analyst. An alert shows a phishing wave that arrived at 07:40 on shipment day. Is that a coincidence? Two-step reasoning — first recognise the tempo detail, then reason about the attacker's goal — turns a strange alert into a clear ATT&CK story: the adversary mapped the tempo (T1591.003) and timed a phishing-for-information wave (T1598) to exploit it.

You will also practice the defender side. The only mitigation ATT&CK lists is M1056 — Pre-compromise: minimise the amount and sensitivity of operational data available to external parties. In practice that means an OSINT exposure review: inventory what you publish, flag rhythm details, remove or generalise them, and re-check. GRC analysts, detection engineers and red teamers all touch this workflow.

All scenarios here are realistic but fictional. Read each artefact carefully: at MEDIUM level the goal is stated, but the path is not — you must decide what to look at first.
