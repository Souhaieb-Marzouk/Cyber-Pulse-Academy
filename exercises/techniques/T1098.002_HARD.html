What you will learn here

This is the demanding tier for T1098.002 — Additional Email Delegate Permissions, the persistence sub-technique of T1098 — Account Manipulation. You already know the mechanism. Here the exercises stop handing you the answer: several options will look defensible, one will be defensible here, and the difference lives in tickets, timestamps, role names and log pairings that you must correlate yourself.

The scenarios mirror the work of senior practitioners. An incident responder at a law firm must not break a litigation hold by yanking a legitimate eDiscovery delegate. A SOC lead at a bank learns that a change ticket can be forged, and must decide what evidence still counts. A detection engineer writes the conditions of the page's analytic AN1051 and hunts tenant-wide for one service account's rights with a single read-only pipeline. A CTI analyst reads the procedure examples the way real teams do: APT29 backdooring a service principal with ApplicationImpersonation during the SolarWinds compromise, Set-CASMailbox allowing attacker devices, HomeLand Justice taking ownership of mailboxes. And a control owner sorts preventive from detective controls instead of calling every control a shield.

Expect three kinds of traps the HARD band promises: something that looks alarming but is benign, something that looks benign but is not, and cases where the correct action is the least aggressive one. Every artefact — audit tables, tickets, role listings, evidence pairs — comes from the official T1098.002 material: Add-MailboxPermission, Default/Anonymous folder roles, Gmail delegation, detection strategy DET0373 with analytics AN1051 and AN1052, and the mitigations M1026, M1032, M1042.

Keywords covered in this exam

These are the ideas the exercises in this file actually test. They all come from the official ATT&CK page for T1098.002.

BEC & incident triage

  • Delegate grant + inbox rule combinations in business email compromise
  • Benign vs suspicious delegation (assistants, helpdesk, eDiscovery)
  • Change-ticket verification and forged approvals

Folder-role abuse

  • Default / Anonymous roles on Top of Information Store or Inbox
  • Any-tenant-account read persistence — the subtlest variant
  • The "external anonymous users" keyword trap

Actor tradecraft from the page

  • APT29 / SolarWinds: global admin → service principal backdoor with ApplicationImpersonation and Mail.Read
  • Set-CASMailbox: attacker devices allowed for ActiveSync
  • HomeLand Justice (C0038): impersonate users, take ownership of mailboxes
  • OWA sign-in pairing with delegate grants (Magic Hound pattern)

Detection engineering

  • AN1051 conditions: Add-MailboxPermission, Default/Anonymous grants, Gmail delegation setup
  • AN1052 conditions: PowerShell execution of Exchange mailbox-permission cmdlets
  • Tenant-wide hunting pipelines; UAL + mailbox audit correlation; time-delta analysis

Response & controls

  • Preserve evidence before containment; least-aggressive correct action
  • Preventive (M1042, M1032, M1026) vs detective (audit reviews, alerts) controls
  • Prioritising containment actions by impact

Terminal work

  • Get-Mailbox piped to Get-MailboxPermission for a tenant-wide hunt
  • Read-only discipline: why the Get verb is the safe verb
