🎓 What you will learn here

This is the EXTREMELY HARD exam for T1591 — Gather Victim Org Information. By now the technique holds no surprises in isolation: an actor reads public material about your organisation — departments, operations, sites, partners, tempo, roles — before touching anything. What remains hard is the judgement layer: deciding what a body of evidence means when it is incomplete, partly explained by legitimate business activity, and being acted on by executives who want a decision in ten minutes.

The exercises put you in that chair. You will weigh a scraping pattern against the marketing campaign that mimics it; you will hold two vendor truths at once (what the public page says, what the internal register says) and decide which one answers the question being asked. You will accept real costs — a press room you choose not to dismantle, an MSP contract you choose not to tear up mid-incident — because the alternatives are worse. You will write confidence language that survives legal review, and you will, at least once, give the answer the CISO did not want: gather more evidence.

The ATT&CK spine is unchanged and every answer still lands on it: collection maps to T1591 and its sub-techniques; the collection’s product feeds Initial Access paths (T1566, T1199, T1195); detection anchors downstream (DET0890); mitigation is M1056’s minimisation discipline. Real procedure context — Volt Typhoon’s patient pre-compromise work, Operation Dream Job’s target identification, Kimsuky’s and APT28’s model-assisted collection — runs through the scenarios as it runs through real ones.

Operating rules

  • Every exercise has three labelled blocks: SCENARIO (blue), QUESTION (amber), YOUR ANSWER (green).
  • Scenarios are long and partly contradictory — that is the job. Collapse panels after reading; the summary line stays.
  • Some artefacts are noise. Some information is missing on purpose. Senior calls are made with both.
  • Hints restate a constraint you may have missed; they never point at the answer. Cost: 10% of that exercise’s credit.
  • Skip and review-skipped work as always; unanswered skipped exercises are excluded from the score.
  • Multi-answer exercises use partial credit: max(0, (H−W)/N). Selecting everything scores zero.
  • Check freezes the timer for unlimited feedback study; Next resumes. Skip and Hint never pause the clock.
  • Several options are defensible in some world. Only one is correct in this world — the constraints decide, not the vocabulary.

One terminal exercise runs on a reduced per-exercise timer (90 seconds) — you will see the countdown in the header when it starts. A safe, defensible command beats a clever one.
