📘 What you will learn here

This is the expert tier for T1591.003 — Identify Business Tempo (parent T1591, tactic Reconnaissance TA0043, platform PRE). The scenarios place you in senior seats: the analyst who must resolve conflicting signals, accept the cost of a correct action, resist a popular best practice when it is wrong here, and write CTI language that survives scrutiny.

You will see rich artefact sets — board mails, tender PDFs, gateway logs, press archives — that include realistic noise and deliberately incomplete information. Some questions have three or more options that are defensible in some world; only one is correct in this world. At least once, the best answer is to gather more evidence or escalate rather than to act.

The ATT&CK frame stays constant: T1591.003 collection happens outside your defences (M1056 — Pre-compromise is the only listed mitigation, and it asks you to minimise exposed operational data). What you decide as a senior responder is what that fact obliges you to do, what it merely suggests, and where the evidence stops.

Hints at this tier only restate a constraint you may have missed — they never point at the answer. Budget your time; several artefacts are long.
