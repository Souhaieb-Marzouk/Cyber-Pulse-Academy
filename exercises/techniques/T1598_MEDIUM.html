📘 What you will learn here

This exam works through T1598 — Phishing for Information (tactic Reconnaissance TA0043, platform PRE) at working depth. You already know the run-versus-reveal split against T1566 and the four sub-techniques (.001 Service, .002 Attachment, .003 Link, .004 Voice). Now you connect the message to the evidence: headers, authentication results, links, sub-technique tells, and the responses that actually help.

The page's real procedure examples anchor the scenarios: APT28 used spearphishing to compromise credentials; Kimsuky used tailored spearphishing to gather victim information including contact lists to identify additional targets; Moonstone Sleet interacted with victims to gather information via email; Scattered Spider paired credential phishing with social engineering to capture one-time codes; ShinyHunters phished Microsoft 365 users for credentials; ZIRCONIUM targeted presidential campaign staffers with credential phishing.

Detection, in the page's own words: monitor for suspicious email activity such as numerous accounts receiving messages from a single unusual or unknown sender; filter on DKIM and SPF or analyse headers to catch spoofed senders; inspect URLs (including expanding shortened links) for references to uncategorised or known-bad sites; monitor social-media traffic for information-requesting messages; and monitor call logs from corporate devices for voice-phishing patterns.

Every exercise here needs two connected steps — read the artefact, then choose what it proves and what to do. The goal is stated; the path is yours.
