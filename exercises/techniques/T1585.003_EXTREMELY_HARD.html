📘What you will learn here

This is the EXTREMELY HARD exam for T1585.003 — Cloud Accounts (sub-technique of T1585 Establish Accounts, Resource Development, platform PRE). The scenarios place you in senior-analyst decisions with incomplete information, conflicting signals and real costs: evidence that will vanish when the provider purges a tenant, a long-standing age signal that points one way while a creation graph points the other, and a committee motion that sounds decisive but targets the wrong phase of the technique.

You will chain the page's knowledge the way an incident commander does: the email-account dependency behind every cloud signup, the created-versus-acquired line that separates T1585.003 from T1586, the three documented campaigns (AkaiRyū payload hosting, Outer Space C2 mailboxes, Storm-1811 Teams impersonation), the detection strategy DET0846 / AN1978 with its exfiltration-stage focus, and the takedown workflow that preserves evidence before the provider acts. Some correct answers are the least aggressive ones; one is "do not touch it yet". Both attacker reasoning (why cloud accounts are cheap, credible and disposable) and defender reasoning (what is observable, what is assessment, what actually bites) are tested — often in the same scenario.
