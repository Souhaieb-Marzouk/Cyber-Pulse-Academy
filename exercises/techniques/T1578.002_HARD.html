ⓘ What you will learn here

▣ YOUR TRAINING GOAL

This is the hard round for T1578.002 — Create Cloud Instance, the Defense Impairment sub-technique of T1578 — Modify Cloud Compute Infrastructure. At this level the exercises stop guiding you. You get an artefact set — logs, tickets, policies, ATT&CK excerpts — and a stakeholder who wants an answer, not a definition. At least two options in every exercise will look defensible; your job is to separate them with evidence: RunInstances creates, StartInstances only powers on; an Auto Scaling service principal creating eight instances is routine, a human role creating one instance in af-south-1 is the incident; an official-looking tag is a claim, a pipeline record is proof.

The scenarios mirror real work. You will triage a noisy CloudTrail queue the way a SOC analyst does at 03:00, harden an analytic the way a detection engineer tunes DET0449, choose the least aggressive containment an incident responder can defend in court, and classify controls the way a GRC analyst maps M1018 and M1047. You will also work with the page's real procedure examples: campaign C0027, where Scattered Spider used a victim's Azure tenant to create VMs, the EC2 creations recorded against G1015, and the credential-first play of LAPSUS$. The traps are the point: the alarming line that is benign, the quiet line that is not, and the aggressive action that would have destroyed your own case.
