🎓 What you will learn here

You will learn what MITRE ATT&CK technique T1591 is and how attackers use it. T1591 is a Technique — in ATT&CK, a technique is one named way an attacker does something. T1591 is called Gather Victim Org Information. It describes an attacker collecting facts about a company as an organisation: its departments, how the business works, and what the key people do. It does not describe stealing passwords or breaking into computers — nothing is hacked yet.

Imagine you are a new analyst in a Security Operations Center (SOC), the team that watches an organisation's systems. Before an attack, an attacker often sits at home and reads public pages: the company website, press releases, job adverts, and LinkedIn profiles. This is called open-source intelligence, or OSINT. With T1591 the attacker learns, for example, which teams exist, who the finance manager is, which vendors the company trusts, or when the company is busiest. That knowledge makes their later phishing emails look real. ATT&CK puts T1591 in the Reconnaissance tactic (TA0043) — the first stage, where the attacker is still only reading and watching.

You will meet this object in many jobs. SOC analysts see its effects when a lure quotes real internal details. Threat-intelligence (CTI) analysts write reports that name T1591 when they describe how a group prepared. Red teamers (eth hackers) do the same collection legally to test you. GRC and awareness teams use it to reduce what the company publishes. Detection engineers use ATT&CK's honest note: this is hard to detect directly, so detection focuses on the attack stages that follow, like the phishing itself.

T1591 has four sub-techniques — smaller chapters under it. In this exam you will learn to recognise the technique, place it in Reconnaissance, name its sub-techniques, spot its public sources, and explain why removing sensitive details from public pages (mitigation M1056 Pre-compromise) is the main defence.

Object basics

  • T1591 = Gather Victim Org Information
  • ATT&CK object type: Technique
  • Sub-techniques: T1591.001, T1591.002, T1591.003, T1591.004
  • Pre-compromise: happens before any system is touched

Tactics & lifecycle

  • Reconnaissance tactic = TA0043
  • First stage of the ATT&CK Enterprise attack lifecycle
  • Feeds later tactics: Initial Access and Resource Development

Sources the adversary reads

  • Company website pages (about, partners, careers)
  • Press releases and job adverts
  • Social media (for example LinkedIn)
  • Search engines and public datasets

What it enables next

  • Tailored phishing (T1566)
  • Trusted Relationship abuse (T1199)
  • Better recon (T1598 Phishing for Information)
  • Operational accounts (T1585)

Defence & detection

  • M1056 Pre-compromise: minimise data available externally
  • Direct detection is hard: activity happens outside your visibility
  • Watch the follow-up stage: targeted phishing and unusual contact attempts

Real procedures you will see

  • Kimsuky collected org hierarchy, functions and press releases
  • Lazarus Group studied public info to tailor spearphishing by department
  • Volt Typhoon did extensive pre-compromise reconnaissance
