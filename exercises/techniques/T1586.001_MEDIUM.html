ⓘ What you will learn here

This unit teaches T1586.001 — Compromise Accounts: Social Media Accounts, a sub-technique of T1586. It is about taking over social media accounts that already exist — profiles on LinkedIn, X or Facebook that real people built and that their contacts trust. The attacker does not create a persona here; the takeover inherits the trust the account already carries.

You will meet it in real work. As a SOC or CTI analyst you track lookalike handles and personas that claim to work for your organisation. As a brand-risk owner you decide when an account goes to the platform for reporting and takedown. In IR you handle the moment a 'colleague' DM turns out to be a hijacked persona. Red teamers rehearse the same moves only inside a signed exercise.

Daily tools: brand-monitor consoles for social media activity about your organisation, platform report and takedown forms, handle-history notes, and monitoring rules from the DET0870 strategy and its analytic AN2002. The page lists one mitigation, M1056 Pre-compromise: enterprise controls cannot stop an outsider's takeover of a third-party account, so monitoring, reporting and staff awareness carry the weight.

The risk to respect: a hijacked persona arrives with real relationships. It needs development — profile edits, new connections, photos — before it is used, often in Spearphishing via Service (T1566.003). Trust is the weapon, and it is invisible to your perimeter.

⚖ Keywords covered in this exam

Adversary behaviour

  • Phishing for Information (T1598) — credential-harvest pretexts
  • buying credentials from third-party broker sites
  • password reuse from breach dumps (brute force)
  • reconnaissance-guided target selection
  • post-takeover persona development: edits, photos, posts

Attack chain

  • long-game social engineering conversations
  • hijacked persona feeds Spearphishing via Service (T1566.003)

Real-world examples & lessons

  • G0065 Leviathan (APT40) — compromised accounts used for social engineering
  • G0034 Sandworm Team — credential-capture webpages against legitimate accounts

Telemetry & detection

  • brand monitoring — DET0870 / AN2002
  • personas claiming to work for your organisation
  • recently modified accounts making numerous connection requests
  • detection focus shifts to Initial Access (T1566.003)

Detection, CTI & IR judgement

  • red hijacks a persona / blue detects the behaviour change
  • verify odd 'colleague' requests through internal channels
