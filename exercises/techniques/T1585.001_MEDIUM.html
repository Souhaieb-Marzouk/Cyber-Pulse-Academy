📘What you will learn here

This exam deepens your mastery of T1585.001 — Social Media Accounts, the sub-technique of T1585 Establish Accounts in the Resource Development tactic (TA0042), run on ATT&CK's PRE platform — before any victim system is touched.

At this level you connect the dots like a working analyst. You will decide why an adversary creates an account instead of stealing one, how a persona is cultivated over weeks (public information, presence, history, affiliations, photos, social networks), and how the finished persona is spent: to build connections directly or through others, to monitor targets, to host C2 nodes, to amplify extortion claims, or to deliver spearphishing through the platform itself.

Real work you will rehearse: triaging a SOC feed and separating persona-building from other alerts, mapping real cases (APT32, CURIUM, HEXANE, EXOTIC LILY, Operation Ghost, Sandworm) to the technique, writing the CTI report line with the right keywords, running your first hunt pivot with a shell command, and explaining to your team lead why the ATT&CK-listed mitigation M1056 Pre-compromise means prevention here is weak — so monitoring and platform reporting carry the load.
