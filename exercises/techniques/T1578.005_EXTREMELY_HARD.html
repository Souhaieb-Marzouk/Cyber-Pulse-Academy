What you will learn here

This is the expert tier for one ATT&CK object: T1578.005 — Modify Cloud Compute Configurations, the sub-technique of T1578 — Modify Cloud Compute Infrastructure (Defense Impairment, TA0112 — the v19 successor of the old Defense Evasion tactic; IaaS) where an adversary with cloud credentials changes account-wide settings — service quotas, subscription associations, tenant-wide policies, allowed regions — so that later abuse fits under limits and outside views, while running instances keep running and no host alarm fires.

Here you work the object the way a senior responder does: inside an unfolding incident with conflicting signals. An approved ticket whose approver no longer stands behind it. A two-year-old automation role that suddenly acts like a person. Region telemetry that simply is not there. You will decide which signal wins and why; you will choose actions whose real business cost you must accept and write down; you will say "do nothing yet — gather more evidence" when containment would cost more than the risk; and you will learn why a widely quoted best practice (deny everything, everywhere, now) is the wrong answer for this specific business. Attribution stays honest: ATT&CK lists no procedure examples for this sub-technique, so confidence stays moderate even when the behaviour is loud.

The roles you exercise are senior ones. You are the IR lead sequencing evidence before remediation; the detection engineer writing the correlated rule and naming its gaps; the CTI lead briefing the CISO with a chain, not a guess; the platform owner redesigning guardrails that survive contact with the business. Everything is anchored to the object: the chain starts at Valid Accounts (T1078), runs through T1578.005, and pays out as Resource Hijacking (T1496) from Unused/Unsupported Cloud Regions (T1535) — exactly the pattern Microsoft Threat Intelligence documented for cryptojacking in 2023. Expect noise, missing pieces, and options that are each defensible in some other incident. In this one, only one path is right.
