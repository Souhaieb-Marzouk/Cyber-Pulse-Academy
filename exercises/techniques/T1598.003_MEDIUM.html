ⓘWhat you will learn here

This exam is a Sub-Technique page from MITRE ATT&CK: T1598.003, Phishing for Information: Spearphishing Link. It sits under its parent technique T1598, Phishing for Information, in the Reconnaissance tactic. In plain words: the adversary sends a targeted email that carries a link, and the message text pushes the reader to click or copy-paste it. The linked page is usually built to look like a portal the target already trusts.

Attackers like links because they are cheap, reusable, and quiet. A link can lead to a cloned login page that collects credentials, or to a relay kit that also takes the session cookie, or to a one-pixel beacon that only reports who opened the mail. Everything collected this way feeds targeting long before any system is touched.

Defenders meet this behaviour everywhere. SOC analysts triage reported mail and hunt click clusters, mail gateway admins tune authentication and link rewriters, threat hunters work the DET0878 detection strategy and its analytic AN2010, CTI analysts map actor procedures, and detection engineers keep URL rules alive in the SIEM and the gateway console.

In daily work you will expand shortened links, read full hostnames out of gateway logs, check how a linked site is categorised, sort real clicks from safe-link noise, write a scoped cluster query, and brief the CISO on which accounts need a reset with session revocation.

The risks are real: credentials and cookies captured today become Valid Accounts later, and beacon data makes the next lure sharper. This exam drills reading a hostname properly, telling information phishing from initial access link phishing, naming the AN2010 signals, sorting obfuscated links, and picking containment steps in an order that keeps the evidence alive.
