🎓 What you will learn here

This exam deepens your knowledge of T1566.004 — Phishing: Spearphishing Voice (vishing), a sub-technique of T1566 Phishing in MITRE ATT&CK, serving the Initial Access tactic. You already know the basics: a caller impersonates a trusted source, applies pressure, and tries to turn one human decision into access to your systems.

In this MEDIUM exam we always combine two ideas. You will read a VoIP/SIP log and map it to the right ATT&CK analytic. You will connect artefacts to the data sources that produce them. You will decide which linked techniques appear in a chain: T1621 (multi-factor authentication request generation — the attacker floods the user's phone with pushes while on the call), T1219 (remote access tools — what the victim is told to install), and the accounts the attacker ends up using. You will also meet the real actors behind the ATT&CK page: the campaign C0027, in which Scattered Spider impersonated IT staff on calls to make victims install a remote monitoring and management (RMM) tool, and Storm-1811, whose IT-support voice calls preceded Black Basta ransomware intrusions.

At work, this is the daily material of a SOC analyst triaging call-plus-execution alerts, a detection engineer implementing the four analytics of detection strategy DET0245 (AN0683 to AN0686), an incident responder containing a session that started with a call, and a CTI analyst writing the campaign note. Every exercise hands you one realistic artefact set and one decision.
