What you will learn here

This is the expert tier for T1583.007 — Acquire Infrastructure: Serverless — the ATT&CK sub-technique (under T1583 — Acquire Infrastructure, tactic Resource Development, platform PRE) covering adversaries purchasing and configuring serverless runtimes such as Cloudflare Workers, AWS Lambda functions and Google Apps Script, then using them to answer infected machines or proxy their traffic to hidden command and control servers while blending into cloud-provider subdomains.

Everything here mirrors a senior chair: senior incident responder, senior detection engineer, CTI lead briefing a CISO. Scenarios chain three or more concepts, and at least one signal in each will contradict another — a change ticket that vouches for one Worker while a beacon runs on another, an EDR network log that disagrees with the proxy, a vendor attribution claim that outruns its evidence. Some artefacts are deliberately noisy or incomplete, because real shifts never hand you a clean picture.

The technique's own ATT&CK notes frame the expert judgement you will practise. Detection (DET0829, analytic AN1961): once a serverless function is in use, hunt for unique characteristics of the adversary software, and focus on related lifecycle stages, because the acquisition happens outside victim visibility. Mitigation (M1056 Pre-compromise): preventive controls cannot easily stop the rental — so the defender's power sits in takedowns, beacon detection, evidence preservation and calibrated reporting. Real cases stay in play: APT41 DUST (C0040) used infrastructure behind Cloudflare and Cloudflare Workers for command and control in 2023–2024, and its tooling (DUSTTRAP S1159, DUSTPAN S1158) shows how implant configuration becomes the pivot that infrastructure analysis lives on.

Expect decisions with real costs — payment downtime, evidence loss, operator tip-off — and expect the widely repeated 'best practice' to be the wrong answer in at least one specific context. If an answer feels easy, reread the artefacts: the trap is always in them.
