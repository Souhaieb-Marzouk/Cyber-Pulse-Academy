🎓What you will learn here

This is the MEDIUM exam for T1091 — Replication Through Removable Media, a MITRE ATT&CK technique: a named way attackers move malware on USB drives and other removable media so it reaches new systems. It lives in the Lateral Movement tactic and also appears in Initial Access, on the Windows platform. The EASY exam taught you the basics; here every exercise connects two ideas — for example, read a log and decide which mitigation answers it.

You will work like a real analyst. A SOC analyst reads mount, file-write and execution events and picks the next action. A detection engineer tunes the analytic on the T1091 page (DET0301 / AN0841) and knows why it fires. A threat-intel analyst maps the groups on the page — APT28, Darkhotel, FIN7, Mustang Panda — to their USB behaviour. A GRC analyst turns the mitigation table (M1040, M1042, M1034) into policy sentences. And a red teamer asks which variant still works after a control lands.

Everything is still written in plain words, but you now decide what to look at first. The goal is stated; the path is not.
