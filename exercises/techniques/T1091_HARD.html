🎓What you will learn here

This is the HARD exam for T1091 — Replication Through Removable Media, the MITRE ATT&CK technique where adversaries copy malware onto USB drives and other removable media so it executes on the next system that uses the drive — including machines on air-gapped networks. It belongs to Lateral Movement and Initial Access, on Windows.

At this level the scenarios look like real casework. You will correlate two to four artefacts — EDR logs, tickets, GPO settings, shell history, ATT&CK excerpts — and pick between answers that are all defensible somewhere else. Some alerts here look scary and are benign; others look clean and are not. At least once, the correct move is the least aggressive one. Real groups from the page — Darkhotel, SHIPSHAPE (APT30), FIN7, Ramsay, QakBot — appear as evidence, not decoration.

Use this exam like a senior SOC or IR person would: name the variant, weigh the controls that are already in place, and choose the action that preserves evidence. The EASY and MEDIUM exams taught the pieces; here the pieces must interact.

📋How this exam works

  • Pass mark: 70% over answered exercises; skipped-and-never-answered exercises are excluded from both sides of the fraction.
  • 15 exercises: log-viewer triage with a filter box, near-miss single choice, multi-select with partial credit, matching, variant-bucket drag-and-drop, ordering of an IR containment sequence, two fill-in word banks, an alert queue simulation, a branching incident, and two terminal tasks (one where you assemble the command yourself, one where you recognise the intruder's command in evidence). The terminals are not adjacent and test different skills.
  • Partial credit: multi-item exercises score max(0, (H − W) / N) — each right item adds one, each wrong item subtracts one.
  • Timer: 50 minutes. Manual Pause hides the exercise. Check auto-stops the timer so you can study the feedback; it resumes on Next.
  • One hint per exercise (−10% of that exercise's credit) removes one wrong path — it never names the answer. Skipped exercises wait in the review pool until the timer ends.
