🎓 What you will learn here

This is the MEDIUM exam for T1591 — Gather Victim Org Information. You already know the basics: T1591 is a Reconnaissance technique where the attacker collects facts about the victim as an organisation — its departments, operations, partners and key people — from public material, before touching any system. Here you go two steps deeper: you will decide which source reveals which detail, and where each detail leads next in an attack.

Think of yourself rotating through three desks for one week. At the CTI desk, you read ATT&CK procedure examples and map what real groups did: Kimsuky collected organisational hierarchy and press releases; Lazarus Group studied public information to aim spearphishing at specific departments; Volt Typhoon ran broad pre-compromise reconnaissance. At the SOC desk, you learn the honest detection story: ATT&CK's detection strategy for T1591 (DET0890) says much of this activity happens outside your visibility with a high false-positive risk, so you anchor detection on the stages that follow, like targeted phishing. At the GRC desk, you review what the company publishes — job adverts, partner pages, leadership bios — through mitigation M1056 Pre-compromise.

The exercises below mix those three desks. You will classify artefacts into the right sub-technique, connect organisation facts to the initial-access techniques they enable (Phishing T1566, Trusted Relationship T1199, Supply Chain Compromise T1195), reason about one red-team choice and its blue-team mirror, and draft report language that names T1591 correctly. Two ideas per exercise is the norm here: identify the fact, then apply it.

Source taxonomy

  • Open web sources (search, social media)
  • Victim-owned websites (careers, partners, press room)
  • Closed sources (paid registries, data brokers)

Where org info leads next

  • Spearphishing (T1566) with credible detail
  • Trusted Relationship abuse (T1199)
  • Supply Chain Compromise (T1195)
  • Establish Accounts (T1585) for persona building

Real procedures

  • Kimsuky (G0094): hierarchy, functions, press releases — plus LLM-assisted collection
  • Lazarus Group (G0032): department-tailored spearphishing
  • FIN7 (G0046): victim lists filtered by revenue via Zoominfo
  • Volt Typhoon (G1017): extensive pre-compromise reconnaissance

Detection strategy reality

  • DET0890 / AN2022: high false-positive rate, activity outside visibility
  • Anchor detection on Initial Access signals that follow recon

Mitigation trade-offs

  • M1056 Pre-compromise: minimise external data
  • Publication reviews (ads, press, partners)
  • What M1056 does NOT cover: post-compromise controls like EDR/MFA
