ⓘ What you will learn here

This unit teaches T1078.003 — Valid Accounts: Local Accounts, a sub-technique of T1078. It is about the abuse of credentials for accounts configured on a single system or service — a workstation's local administrator, a device's root, a service identity — managed on the host itself, not by a domain controller. Password reuse of one local admin credential can open a whole fleet.

You will meet it in real work. As a SOC analyst you triage local logons that break the account's own pattern: an off-hours console session, a service identity at the keyboard, a rare SSH sign-in. As an IR lead you connect a credential-dumping alert to the local admin password that then moved across hosts. As a detection engineer you tune the DET0407 strategy and its analytics AN1137, AN1138 and AN1139. As IT support you run the hygiene that stops reuse: unique local admin passwords through LAPS, permission audits and the removal of unused accounts. As a red teamer you exercise local-account paths only inside a signed authorization.

Daily tools: SIEM queries over 4624/4625 and Linux auth logs, EDR consoles, SID anchors such as the -500 Administrator RID, and control checklists for M1032 MFA, M1027 password policies, M1026 privileged account management (including LAPS and Kubernetes service accounts) and M1018 user account management.

The risk to respect: one shared local admin password turns one theft into a fleet-wide path, dumped local credentials feed reuse, and valid logons blend with normal work — under Stealth (the defense-evasion tactic) this abuse hides. Dormant or newly created local accounts persist quietly and survive rebuilds.

⚖ Keywords covered in this exam

Object basics & boundaries

  • built-in locals: Administrator RID -500, Guest -501, NT AUTHORITY\SYSTEM

Adversary behaviour

  • password reuse: one local admin password, many hosts
  • credential dumping from local SAM/LSASS feeds reuse
  • lateral movement with local admin (PsExec/wmic)
  • brute forcing a local admin password
  • service accounts as local credentials (incl. Kubernetes)

Telemetry & detection (DET0407 family)

  • AN1137 — not normally interactive, or outside business hours
  • AN1138 — outside the expected operational context
  • AN1139 — rare local logons via SSH
  • DET0407 — behaviour first, for initial access and persistence

Mitigations & controls

  • M1027 — complex, unique local admin passwords
  • M1026 — permission audits, LAPS, Kubernetes service accounts
  • M1018 — remove inactive and unused local accounts

Real-world procedure examples

  • G0016 APT29 — dormant accounts
  • S0367 Emotet — brute force, then move
  • G0046 FIN7 / G0125 HAFNIUM — SYSTEM on Exchange
  • G0056 PROMETHIUM — creates admin accounts
  • S0368 NotPetya — PsExec/wmic spread

Detection & IR judgement

  • red reuses the local admin password
  • blue catches the multi-host pattern
