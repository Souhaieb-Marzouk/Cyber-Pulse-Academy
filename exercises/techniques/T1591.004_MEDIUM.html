📘 What you will learn here

This exam works through T1591.004 — Identify Roles (parent T1591 — Gather Victim Org Information, tactic Reconnaissance TA0043, platform PRE) at working depth. You already know it collects identities and roles — including what data and resources each role can access. Here you connect the role map to the pretext, the wave that follows, and the controls that blunt both.

The ATT&CK page carries real procedure examples, and you will meet them here: FIN7 identified IT staff and employees with higher administrative rights; HEXANE identified executives, HR and IT staff at victim organisations for further targeting; LAPSUS$ gathered detailed knowledge of team structures; Volt Typhoon identified key network and IT staff pre-compromise. Different motives — crime, espionage, disruption — same recon technique.

In daily work this object is a team sport. CTI analysts tag the role-targeting in group reports; detection engineers predict which mailboxes will be impersonated and tune mailbox-level analytics; GRC analysts run the minimisation review over people pages, job adverts and speaker bios; IR teams scope incidents by asking “which roles were approached first?”. You will practice all four views.

Every exercise needs two connected steps: recognise the role-relevant fact, then reason about what it enables or how to reduce it. The goal is stated; the path is yours to choose.
