📘 What you will learn here

This is the challenging tier for T1591.004 — Identify Roles (parent T1591, tactic Reconnaissance TA0043, platform PRE). The scenarios are realistic tickets with several artefacts, partial information and near-miss answers: options that are defensible in a different context but wrong in this one.

The real procedure examples on the ATT&CK page anchor the scenarios: FIN7 identified IT staff and employees with higher administrative rights; HEXANE identified executives, HR and IT staff at victim organisations for further targeting; LAPSUS$ gathered detailed knowledge of team structures; Volt Typhoon identified key network and IT staff pre-compromise. You will map observations to these behaviours and to the correct technique — and refuse mappings the evidence does not support.

Defender work at this tier means three habits: spotting the quiet leak (a single access note added to an ordinary page), choosing the least aggressive effective response, and matching each control to what it can and cannot do — M1056 minimises what is published; good controls like MFA still do not stop the collection itself.

Expect at least one exercise where the obvious answer is wrong for a specific reason, and one where the right action is to warn the named people rather than to 'do security theatre'. Read every artefact before you commit.
