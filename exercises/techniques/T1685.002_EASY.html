🎓 What you will learn here

  • This exam is about a MITRE ATT&CK sub-technique: T1685.002 Disable or Modify Cloud Log, which belongs to technique T1685 Disable or Modify Tools and the Defense Impairment tactic.
  • You will learn what cloud logging is and why attackers switch it off: AWS CloudTrail, Microsoft 365 audit, Okta System Log, and SaaS admin panels.
  • You will learn the real methods: StopLogging, DeleteTrail, removing SNS topics, disabling multi-region logging, disabling log-file validation, Set-MailboxAuditBypassAssociation, license downgrades, and stopping SaaS log exports.
  • You will learn who does this in the real world: APT29 disabled Purview Audit before stealing emails, and the tool Pacu can restrict AWS logging.
  • You will learn how defenders catch it (detection strategy DET0289) and how mitigation M1018 User Account Management limits who may change logging.
  • By the end of this exam you can name the sub-technique, its parent, its tactic, its platforms, and simple examples of cloud log tampering.
