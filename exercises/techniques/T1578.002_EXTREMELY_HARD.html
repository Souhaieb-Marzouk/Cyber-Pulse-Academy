ⓘ What you will learn here

▣ YOUR TRAINING GOAL

This is the expert round for T1578.002 — Create Cloud Instance. The exercises here behave like the decisions a senior incident responder, detection engineer, or CTI lead actually loses sleep over. Nothing arrives clean: signals contradict each other (a passed vulnerability scan against an impossible identity; a signed change ticket against a foreign source IP; official-looking tags against an empty pipeline record), information is incomplete (no geolocation, VPN, unreachable approvers), and the correct action sometimes costs something real — money, evidence, or a Tuesday night.

You will chain three or more concepts per exercise: the identity step (valid accounts, token theft), the creation step (RunInstances / virtualMachines/write / instances.insert), the sibling steps (T1578.001 snapshots feeding the new machines, T1578.003 cleanup), and the control stack that ends the story (M1018 least privilege, M1047 audit, region guardrails, just-in-time elevation). The analytic thread is DET0449 / AN1242: rare accounts, odd geolocations, rapid snapshot-then-create sequences, and unexpected policy changes on fresh instances — tuned with IaC allowlists and change tickets. The scenarios expect you to say which signal wins and why, to refuse a wrong best practice even when a CISO demands it, to name the evidence that dies when you press the button, and to know when the right answer is contain, preserve, and escalate. That is the job. This exam makes you rehearse it.
