ⓘ What you will learn here

This exam covers T1683.001 — Generate Content: Written Content, a sub-technique of T1683 Generate Content. It sits in the Resource Development tactic (TA0042) on the PRE platform: the writing happens before or during an operation, outside the victim's estate. The sub-technique covers written materials adversaries create or tailor to support operations — phishing lures, fraudulent financial and BEC letters, fabricated job postings and employment credentials, decoy documents, persona content, and the sustaining narratives that keep a fake identity believable over time. Authorship is manual, commissioned from third parties, or AI-assisted: AI translates content into the target's native language, imitates the communication style of trusted senders, and researches targets to build pretexts. The boundary matters: malicious code and scripts are out of scope here — that is T1587 Develop Capabilities.

As a CTI analyst you will cluster lure texts, correlate them to groups and kits, and brief defenders. The page's procedure examples give you real anchors: APT-C-36 mails impersonating official notifications, APT38 decoy content resembling legitimate support guidance, Contagious Interview fake LinkedIn and Telegram personas, NIMBUS MANTICORE career-themed lures for aerospace and defense, TeamPCP Dune-themed repositories built with stolen tokens, The Gentlemen branded ransom notes, and the Kali365 PhaaS kit whose AI evaluates victim mails and generates tailored responses that carry BEC forward.

In incident response you will handle forged documents like the C0064 case, where an adversary tasked Claude Code to generate forged tax compliance certificates from data collected by custom python scripts. Your job there is to contain the live access first, preserve the artefacts with full headers, verify through published channels — never the contacts printed inside the forgery — and then feed notification and takedown.

As a detection engineer you learn the lesson of M1056 Pre-compromise: no preventive control stops a person from writing text outside your estate, so defenses must not rely on atomic indicators such as single domains or addresses. DET0917 and its analytic AN2060 point detection downstream, at related lifecycle stages such as Initial Access: mail gateway hits, brand monitoring, worker reports, takedown requests. You will pick behavioural anchors — like urgency plus payment-change semantics — over style scores that AI mimicry defeats.

Red teamers use this sub-technique to write authorized, realistic lures for awareness exercises and document the content lifecycle so blue teams can tune against it. Work through the fifteen HARD exercises: correlate artefacts, resist the plausible shortcut, and expect the obvious answer to be wrong more than once.
