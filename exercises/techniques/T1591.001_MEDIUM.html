🎓 What you will learn here

This is the MEDIUM exam for T1591.001 — Determine Physical Locations, the T1591 sub-technique that collects where the victim physically is: offices, plants, data centers — and, through those places, the legal jurisdiction and authorities the organisation answers to.

Here you connect the places to what they enable. The sub-technique’s description names the downstream uses explicitly: more reconnaissance, operational-resource decisions (Develop Capabilities T1587 / Obtain Capabilities T1588 informed by jurisdiction), and initial access through Phishing (T1566) or Hardware Additions (T1200). The documented procedure stays Magic Hound’s — location harvested from visitors to its phishing sites — a reminder that this collection can run entirely on the adversary’s infrastructure.

The exercises rotate three desks. At the CTI desk you separate T1591.001 from its siblings (tempo, roles) with evidence. At the SOC desk you read logs and facility reports and learn which signals are collection and which are life. At the GRC/facility desk you run the minimisation review — city versus street, announcements versus readiness — and meet the dual perspective: the same site page is the red team’s map and your exposure.

Two-step reasoning is the norm: name the artefact’s fact, then name where it leads. One exercise lets you type a real command in the simulated shell; one walks a live facility branch.
