🎓What you will learn here

This is the EXTREMELY HARD exam for T1091 — Replication Through Removable Media, the MITRE ATT&CK technique for moving malware across systems on USB drives and similar media — including air-gapped networks no packet can cross. Tactics: Lateral Movement and Initial Access. Platform: Windows.

These fifteen exercises are senior-level casework: four or more artefacts, realistic noise, missing telemetry, and signals that contradict each other. You will decide which signal wins and say why; you will accept actions with real business cost; you will find the exercise where the industry best practice is the wrong answer; and at least once the best move is to gather more evidence instead of acting. The cast comes from the T1091 page: Stuxnet's LNK trick (CVE-2010-2568), APT28's USB tooling, FIN7's BadUSB, Tropic Trooper's USBferry, HIUPAN, Gamaredon and more.

You are the senior analyst here. The CISO wants a decision in ten minutes; the plant manager wants no downtime; legal wants evidence that survives scrutiny. Balance all three — that is the exam.
