🎓 What you will learn here

This is the HARD tier for T1574.006 (the ATT&CK sub-technique for hijacking dynamic linker variables on Linux and macOS). The exercises behave like real Unix casework — including the uncomfortable part: on a hooked host, your own tools may lie to you.

You will work the page's artifact-scrubbing facts (hooking execve and readdir hides malware from ls, ldd, iptables, dmesg), the macOS DYLD_FRAMEWORK_PATH / DYLD_LIBRARY_PATH variables, the M1028 restriction toolkit (SIP, Hardened Runtime, setuid/setgid bits, entitlements, __RESTRICT), and detection engineering for DET0435 (AN1209 + AN1210) with baselines for legitimate developer/debug use.

The page's harder procedures are in play: COATHANGER (S1105) injecting into PID 1 and swapping the reboot function, Ebury (S0377) hooking seven libc functions from SSH sessions, Rocke (G0106) hiding droppers and miners in process lists, and SPAWNCHIMERA (S9024) compiled as a PIE with a third-party injection library.

Hints remove one wrong path — they never name the answer. When the on-host output looks too clean, that is not reassurance; it is a finding.
