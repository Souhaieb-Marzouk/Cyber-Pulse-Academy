📘What you will learn here

This exam deepens your mastery of T1585.002 — Email Accounts (sub-technique of T1585 Establish Accounts, Resource Development, PRE). At this level you combine two facts per exercise: what the adversary did, and which ATT&CK object or control it maps to.

You will work through the mailbox's double life: a registration identity for domains and free services (the page's infrastructure sentences), a sender identity built for phishing (T1566/T1598), a persona's contact point paired with social media accounts, and the extortion desk of a ransomware operation. The real examples carry it: APT42's spearphishing accounts, Kimsuky's phishing operations, Mustang Panda's abuse of the legitimate SMTP2Go marketing service plus fake Google accounts and Proton Mail, MirrorFace's Gmail mailboxes in Operation AkaiRyū. On the defensive side you will practise the honest scope: inbound controls such as SPF/DKIM/DMARC blunt the use of lookalike senders — nothing enterprise-side stops the creation, which is why DET0835/AN1967 points detection at Initial Access.
