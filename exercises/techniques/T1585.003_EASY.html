📘What you will learn here

You will learn T1585.003 — Cloud Accounts, the cloud sub-technique of T1585 Establish Accounts in the Resource Development tactic. Before an attack, the adversary registers accounts with cloud providers — storage services like Dropbox, MEGA, Microsoft OneDrive or AWS S3 buckets, and full cloud platforms like Microsoft 365 — and later uses them to host tools and stolen data, to build command channels, or to rent servers without running any of their own.

Imagine you are a new analyst: the "shared document" your colleague received last month came from a OneDrive account that was created the week before. That account is this technique. In real work you will meet it in cloud audit logs (new external tenants and shares), in incident response (where the attacker's cloud account acted against your users), and in detection engineering (because ATT&CK's own guidance says the creation itself is invisible — you detect the use). The page also teaches a key dependency: cloud accounts need an e-mail account first, which links this technique to its sibling T1585.002. ATT&CK marks it PRE, and the listed mitigation M1056 Pre-compromise tells you prevention here is weak.
