1What you will learn here

This is the top tier for T1647 — Plist File Modification, the macOS Technique in ATT&CK's Defense Impairment tactic (TA0005). Here the exercises mirror a senior analyst's worst Tuesday: four artefacts, one signal that contradicts another, one host with no telemetry, and a CISO who needs a decision in ten minutes.

The scenario: a design studio's Mac fleet shows hidden helpers, a notarised Dock helper abuse, and an LSEnvironment injection set-up — the full T1647 playbook that ATT&CK documents through XCSSET (S0658) and Cuckoo Stealer (S1153). Around it: an MDM push that could explain everything, a signature that looks clean, and business pressure to 'just reimage tonight'.

You will practise the three skills this tier exists for. Chain reasoning: your answer at step four depends on the call you made at step one. Conflict resolution: when two signals contradict, you must say which one wins and why — managed preferences against a 02:14 user-context plutil, for example. Cost acceptance: sometimes the right action costs real downtime or loses a shortcut, and you must choose it anyway; sometimes the widely praised 'best practice' is exactly wrong here.

This maps to real senior work: leading the technical side of a macOS incident as the incident responder and SOC analyst escalation point, sweeping the fleet as a threat hunter, tuning the analytic with the detection engineer, briefing a CISO in one page, making the attribution-or-not call as a CTI lead, and choosing evidence-preserving commands under pressure — because a senior's mistakes are expensive, and so are their good decisions.

3How this exam works

This file contains 16 exercises at EXTREMELY HARD level: three or more chained concepts, conflicting signals, deliberate noise, and missing information you must reason around.
