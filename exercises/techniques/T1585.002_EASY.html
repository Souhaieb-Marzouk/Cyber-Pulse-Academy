📘What you will learn here

You will learn T1585.002 — Email Accounts, the e-mail sub-technique of T1585 Establish Accounts in the Resource Development tactic. Before an attack, the adversary registers fresh e-mail mailboxes — at big free providers, at privacy-focused providers, or at disposable ("burner") services — and later uses them as the sender identity for phishing, for extortion, for registering domains, and as the contact point of a fake persona.

Imagine you are a new analyst: last month's invoice scam came from a mailbox that was created three weeks earlier and impersonated a supplier. That mailbox is this technique. In real work you will meet it in the mail gateway (first-contact senders), in CTI reports (sender addresses written defanged, like name AT provider [.] com), in brand-protection takedowns, and in awareness training about fake job offers and invoice fraud. ATT&CK marks the technique PRE: the mailbox is created on the attacker's side, before anyone in your company is touched — which is also why the listed mitigation, M1056 Pre-compromise, tells you prevention here is weak.
