📘 What you will learn here

This is the expert tier for T1591.004 — Identify Roles (parent T1591, tactic Reconnaissance TA0043, platform PRE). You will practice the four senior reflexes this technique demands: resolving conflicting signals (public-source explanation vs internal-leak hypothesis), accepting the cost of a correct action, refusing a popular best practice when it is wrong here, and choosing gather-more-evidence / escalate when the evidence is not there. What changes at this tier is judgement: what to assert in the board note, what to verify at a price, and what to leave as an open question with a named owner.
