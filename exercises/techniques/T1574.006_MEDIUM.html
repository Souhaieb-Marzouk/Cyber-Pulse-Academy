🎓 What you will learn here

You already know the basics of T1574.006 (the ATT&CK sub-technique for hijacking dynamic linker variables on Linux and macOS). This exam adds the second reasoning step.

You will practise combining evidence with page facts: when the linker reads the variables (execution-preparation phase), why developers use them legitimately, the sshd descendant-injection example, how a hooked execve replaces the system call, the file-based /etc/ld.so.preload variant, and the page's detection strategy DET0435 with analytics AN1209 (Linux) and AN1210 (macOS).

You will also apply the page's OS-configuration mitigation M1028 (SIP ignores DYLD_* for protected binaries) and work with the page's procedure table: Aquatic Panda, HiddenWasp, Hildegard, Rocke and MEDUSA.
