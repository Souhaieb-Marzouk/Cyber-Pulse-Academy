The Vendor Risk Review Binder

Meridian Vale Health Network (fictional) has a problem: every day, outside organizations touch its systems, data and patients. You are the third-party risk analyst who decides which vendors are safe enough to trust — and how MVHN will know, month after month. This lab exercises every concept of CompTIA Security+ SY0-801 objective 5.3 (Domain 5 — Security Program Management & Oversight, 20% of the exam).

Guided

Medium — The Q3 Review Cycle

Run the full annual vendor risk review, phase by phase, with the binder guiding you at every step.

  • 12 guided objectives across 5 phases of the review cycle
  • Up to 3 tiered hints per objective (nudge → direction → demonstration)
  • Tolerant checking: synonyms accepted, every wrong answer teaches
  • Concept quiz with explanations after each answer
War Room

Hard — The Peregrine Incident

A courier vendor reports a ransomware event at its subcontractor. Convene the war room: triage noisy evidence, find the gaps, decide the response.

  • 10 broad, multi-step challenges on one incident desk
  • 7 evidence items — including two red herrings that look urgent
  • Strict validation: exact values, exact sets, exact sequence
  • No hints by default — one optional nudge that visibly costs score
  • Scenario quiz: every item demands a decision and a justification
