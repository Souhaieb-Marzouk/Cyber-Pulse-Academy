Open a job ticket

One drafting room, two ways to work it. Both modes use the same evidence — they differ in guidance, validation strictness and how much noise is buried in the files. Progress is tracked separately per mode.

Guided Drafting — Medium

  • 12 granular objectives, suggested work order with “where to look” pointers
  • 3-tier hints available freely (they cost stars, never progress)
  • Tolerant checks: reasonable formats accepted, every answer teaches
  • 6-question concept quiz with explanations

Unassisted Review — Hard

  • 9 broad multi-step challenges; no pointers, no “where to look”
  • Evidence contains distractors and at least two false alarms
  • Strict checks: exact values, exact sets, a hard budget gate
  • 7 scenario items requiring justification; efficiency counts

Everything is saved in your browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere. Technical terms are defined in the Field Manual and the Glossary folder — dotted-underlined words in the sheets link straight to them.

Scenario briefing — synthetic training data

Meridian Cascades Grocery Co-op is losing its datacenter

SituationThe lease on the Eastport DC ends June 30 — 21 days out. Every production workload must be assessed, specified and moved — or explicitly not moved — before then.
Your roleYou are the migration architect. The co-op is moving to a fictional provider, Nimbus Cloud, over a dedicated 1 Gbps link with a 4-hour nightly migration window.
Your deliverableA Requirements Dossier: a stamped specification sheet per workload, transfer math on the Bandwidth Ledger, a wave plan, and strategy decisions — the full work of analyzing system requirements for workload migrations (CV0-004 objective 2.1).
Why it mattersDomain 2.0 Deployment carries 19% of the CV0-004 exam, and objective 2.1 is its first step: skip the analysis and every later migration task is guesswork.
All organizations, products, prices, logs and people in this room are fictional training material. Numbers are internally consistent teaching values, not vendor quotes.
