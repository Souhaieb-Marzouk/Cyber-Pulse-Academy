The Night-Shift Depot
Configuration Management Bench

Kestrel Valley Transit Authority runs its network from a depot control desk. Tonight you hold the duty phone. Three configuration types — production, backup and baseline — decide whether Monday morning runs on time. Aligned to CompTIA Network+ N10-009 configuration & change management content (Domain 3.0).

Guided night shift

Medium — understand & learn. The shift lead walks the desk with you: 11 graded duty items, each with context, a “where to look” pointer and up to three hint lamps.

  • 11 guided objectives, in order
  • Tiered hints (cost stars, never block)
  • Tolerant checking — wrong answers teach
  • Quiz after practice · supportive tone

Solo incident response

Hard — test & stretch. 07:12 Monday: fare-gate payments are failing and the logs went quiet. No shift lead. You triage noisy evidence with red herrings and defend every call.

  • 9 deep, multi-step challenges
  • Strict validation — near-misses rejected with rationale
  • At most one nudge per item, visibly scored
  • Analysis quiz with justification

Both modes share the same depot, the same devices and the same evidence core. Progress auto-saves in your browser. Nothing leaves this page.
