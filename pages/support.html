Support CyberPulseAcademy

CyberPulseAcademy is free, has no adverts and takes no money from vendors. The material is written by one author in his own time, and the only thing that limits how much of it exists is how much time he can afford to spend on it. Voluntary support buys that time. It is never required, it never unlocks anything, and nothing on this site is withheld from anyone who cannot or does not want to give.

If you are a student, between contracts, or simply do not have spare money, please keep studying and ignore this page entirely. That is a completely reasonable choice and it does not make you a second-class user of anything here.

What the money funds

This is a small project with no salaries, no office and no marketing budget, so the honest answer is that support buys one thing: time from people who already have full-time jobs.

Time to write and review batches

A single exercise batch is not a paragraph of prose. It is questions with defensible answers, distractors that are wrong for a specific reason, written explanations, a scoring contract and an accessibility pass. Writing one takes hours; reviewing someone else's takes almost as long. This is where nearly all of the money goes.

Accuracy review against official sources

Technical claims are checked against primary documentation and public framework data before publication, because study material that is subtly wrong is worse than no material. That review is unpaid work made possible by funded time.

Accessibility testing on real devices

Testing with a screen reader and with keyboard-only navigation is not something a desktop browser emulator can do for you. Support pays for the device time to check the site on iOS Safari and on Android Chrome, on real hardware, on poor connections.

Hosting costs, which are close to zero

The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. There is no application server, no database and no paid runtime. Hosting is effectively free, so money given here does not disappear into infrastructure, and this page will say so for as long as that remains true.

What this is, in compliance terms

Voluntary personal support. Not a purchase. Not tax-deductible.

Donations are processed by PayPal. CyberPulseAcademy never sees or stores your card details.

CyberPulseAcademy does not claim charitable, nonprofit or tax-exempt status in any jurisdiction, and it does not issue donation receipts. A donation here buys nothing: no content, no feature, no priority, no removal of a limit, and no influence over what gets written. It is a voluntary personal contribution from one person to another, given because you think the work is worth continuing. If your tax rules require a registered charity for a deduction, this is not one and you should not treat it as one.

Because a third party processes the payment, PayPal's Acceptable Use Policy applies to the transaction, including its restrictions on certain fundraising categories, and PayPal's own terms govern the payment itself. Nothing on this page is legal or tax advice, and nothing here is a promise about how any particular payment will be treated in your country. If that matters to your decision, treat it as a reason not to give and keep using the study material, which stays free either way.

Placement strategy

How a project asks for money says as much about it as whether it asks at all. These are the rules CyberPulseAcademy holds itself to, published so that breaking one is visible rather than a matter of taste.

One quiet footer link

A single, small link in the footer of every page. It is not a banner, it is not sticky, it does not follow the scroll position and it never covers content.

This one dedicated page

Everything explanatory lives here, where you arrived deliberately. No other page repeats the details or re-asks.

One post-pass celebration card

On a topic page, one card may appear after you pass a batch, and only after a pass. Never before, never on a failure, and never more than once per topic page.

Never a mid-exam interruption

Nothing is asked while you are working through a batch. No modal, ever. No content is gated behind a donation, and no feature is withheld to create pressure to give.
